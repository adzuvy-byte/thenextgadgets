*Last updated: 3 November 2026*

This policy explains what information we and our partners collect when you visit TheNextGadgets (thenextgadgets.com), and the choices you have. We keep data collection to a minimum.

## What we collect

**Information you give us.** We do not ask you to create an account or to sign up. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and we use it only to reply and to improve the site.

**Information collected automatically.** Like most websites, our hosting provider, Google Analytics and our advertising partners may collect standard technical information, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, language, referring page, pages visited and the time spent on them. We use this to run the site, understand which articles help readers and keep the site secure.

## Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site, such as which pages they read, how they found us and what device they use. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies to collect this information, and Google processes it under its own policies. You can opt out by installing the [Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout) or by blocking cookies in your browser.

## Cookies and similar technologies

We and our partners may use cookies and similar technologies to measure traffic, remember settings and show ads. See our [cookie policy](/cookie-policy/) for details and how to control them.

## Advertising

We may show ads from Google AdSense and other ad networks. These partners may use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies lets it and its partners show ads based on your visit to our site and other sites on the internet.

You can manage personalised ads in [Google's Ad Settings](https://adssettings.google.com), and learn more about how Google uses data at [Google's privacy and terms](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' personalised advertising at [aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info).

## Affiliate links

Some links on our site go to Amazon.in and other retailers. When you click one, the retailer may set its own cookies to record that you came from our site, so that we can be credited for a qualifying purchase. We do not receive your account details or payment information. The retailer's own privacy policy applies once you are on its site.

## How we use information

- to run, secure and improve the site;
- to understand which content is useful;
- to show ads and earn affiliate commissions that fund the site;
- to reply to messages you send us;
- to comply with the law.

We do not sell your personal information.

## Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers that help us run the site (such as hosting, analytics and advertising partners) and when the law requires it. These providers process data under their own policies.

## Retention

Technical and analytics data is kept by our providers for the periods in their own settings. Emails you send us are kept as long as needed to deal with your message.

## Your choices and rights

- Block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or use a private window.
- Opt out of personalised ads using the links above.
- Ask us what personal data we hold from your emails, and ask us to correct or delete it, by writing to us on the [contact page](/contact-us/). Under Indian data protection law you have rights over your personal data, and we will respond to valid requests.

## Children

TheNextGadgets is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you think a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

## Other sites

Our articles link to other sites. We do not control them and are not responsible for their privacy practices.

## Changes

We may update this policy as the site changes. The date at the top shows when it was last revised.
