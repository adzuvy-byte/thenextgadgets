TheNextGadgets helps buyers in India choose gadgets and home appliances with confidence. We publish buying guides, comparisons, reviews and fix-it guides for products people actually buy: TVs, air conditioners, refrigerators, dashcams, chargers, laptops, phones and more.

## What we are trying to do

Most product advice online is either a list of affiliate links or a rewrite of a manufacturer's spec sheet. We want to be the page you open before you buy: clear about what matters, honest about what we do not know, and easy to act on.

- **We write for Indian buyers.** Models, prices, warranties, power rates and service networks are different here, and our advice reflects that.
- **We explain trade-offs.** Every pick lists who it suits and who should skip it.
- **We say what we have not tested.** Unless an article says we tested a product, specifications come from the maker or the retailer listing, and we mark claims that are the maker's own.

## Who writes here

Articles are written by our editorial team and by our writers, Lucilla (home and kitchen appliances) and Daniel (cars and dashcams). You can see every writer's articles on their author page. Each article shows when it was last updated, and we revisit buying guides when models change.

## How we make money

The site is free to read. We earn a commission when you buy through some of our links, as a participant in the Amazon Associates programme, and we may show ads. Neither changes what we recommend. The details are in our [affiliate disclosure](/disclaimer/) and our [editorial policy](/editorial-policy/).
