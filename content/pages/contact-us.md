We read every message and reply as soon as we can, usually within a few working days.

**Email:** [hello@thenextgadgets.com](mailto:hello@thenextgadgets.com)

## What to write to us about

- **A mistake or an outdated article.** Please send the link to the article and what looks wrong. We correct confirmed errors and update the "last updated" date. See our [editorial policy](/editorial-policy/).
- **A product you want us to cover.** Tell us the model and what you want to know. We cannot promise to cover every request.
- **A question about buying.** We are happy to point you to the right article, but we cannot give personal recommendations for every situation.
- **Business or advertising enquiries.** Write "Business" in the subject line. We keep editorial and commercial decisions separate, so a partnership never buys a recommendation or a good rating.

## What we cannot help with

- **Orders, delivery, returns and refunds.** We do not sell products. Contact the retailer or brand directly.
- **Warranty and repairs.** Contact the brand's authorised service centre with your invoice and serial number.
- **Free products or paid reviews.** We do not publish paid reviews. If a brand sends a product for testing, we say so in the article.
