*Last updated: 3 November 2026*

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. This page explains how they may be used on TheNextGadgets. It should be read with our [privacy policy](/privacy-policy/).

## How cookies may be used here

- **Essential and functional.** Needed for the site to work or to remember a setting, for example a display preference.
- **Analytics.** We use Google Analytics to understand how many people visit and which articles they read, so we can improve the site. You can opt out with the [Google Analytics opt-out add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).
- **Advertising.** Ad partners such as Google AdSense may set cookies to show ads, limit how often you see an ad and measure ad performance, including ads based on your visits to this and other sites.
- **Affiliate.** When you click an affiliate link, the retailer (for example Amazon.in) may set cookies to record that you came from our site so we can be credited for a qualifying purchase.

Third-party cookies are set by those partners and are covered by their own policies.

## Your choices

- **Browser settings.** You can block or delete cookies in your browser. Some features may not work as well, and you may still see ads that are not personalised.
- **Personalised ads.** Manage Google's ad personalisation at [adssettings.google.com](https://adssettings.google.com), or opt out of some third-party vendors at [aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info).
- **Private browsing.** A private window clears cookies when you close it.

## Changes

We may update this policy if the cookies we or our partners use change. The date at the top shows the latest version.
