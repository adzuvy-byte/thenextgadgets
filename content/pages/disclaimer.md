## Affiliate disclosure

TheNextGadgets is a participant in the Amazon Associates programme, an affiliate advertising programme designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by advertising and linking to Amazon.in. **As an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases.**

In practice: when you click a link to a product on Amazon.in from our site and then buy that product or other eligible items in the same visit, we may earn a small commission. **It costs you nothing extra.** The price you pay is the same with or without our link.

### How this affects what we write

- Commissions never decide what we recommend, how we rank products or how we rate them.
- We do not take payment for placement or ratings.
- We may recommend products that are not available on Amazon, and we may list products with no affiliate link.
- If a brand sends us a product, or we have any other relationship with a brand, we say so in the article.

Affiliate links usually go to Amazon.in and carry our Associates tag. You can see the destination by hovering over a link.
