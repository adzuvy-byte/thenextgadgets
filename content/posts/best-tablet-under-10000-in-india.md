## How to choose a budget tablet

- **Screen:** a Full HD (1920 x 1200) display is better for video and reading than 1280 x 800.
- **RAM and storage:** 2GB RAM is enough for video and reading. For apps and multitasking, look for 3GB or 4GB, and 32GB storage with a microSD slot.
- **Battery:** most budget tablets list around 5,000 mAh, which is enough for a day of streaming.
- **Wi-Fi or 4G:** most are Wi-Fi only. Choose a 4G model like the Lenovo Tab M7 only if you need a SIM card.
- **Software updates:** budget tablets get few updates, so check the Android version.
