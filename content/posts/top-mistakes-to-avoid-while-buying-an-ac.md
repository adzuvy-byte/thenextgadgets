To help you make a smarter choice, we’ve listed the **top mistakes to avoid while buying an AC**. Whether it’s your first AC or a replacement, avoid these common pitfalls and stay cool — literally and financially!

## 1. Ignoring the Correct Tonnage for Your Room

**Mistake:** Buying a 1-ton AC for a large room or a 2-ton AC for a small space.

**Why it's wrong:** AC tonnage determines the cooling capacity. A mismatch will either overwork the unit (high bills, poor cooling) or be unnecessarily expensive.

**Quick Guide:**

- Up to 120 sq. ft → 1 Ton
- 121–180 sq. ft → 1.5 Ton
- 181–250 sq. ft → 2 Ton

👉 *Always measure your room size before selecting AC tonnage.*

## 2. Overlooking Inverter Technology

**Mistake:** Choosing a non-inverter AC just to save money upfront.

**Why it's wrong:** Non-inverter ACs consume more power in the long run and are less efficient. Inverter ACs regulate compressor speed and offer better cooling with lower electricity consumption.

✅ *Go for an inverter AC if you plan to use it frequently or for long hours.*

## 3. Ignoring Energy Star Ratings

**Mistake:** Picking an AC without checking its **BEE star rating**.

**Why it's wrong:** The higher the rating (up to 5 stars), the more energy-efficient the AC. A 3-star AC might cost less initially, but a 5-star AC will save more in electricity bills.

⚡ *Tip: Check the BEE star label on the unit, and make sure it shows the current rating, since star standards are revised from time to time.*

## 4. Not Considering the Climate and Room Conditions

**Mistake:** Buying the same AC model recommended by a friend from another region.

**Why it's wrong:** Coastal areas, dry zones, and humid climates have different cooling needs. Rooms with large windows, poor insulation, or direct sunlight need more powerful ACs.

🌡️ *Customize your choice based on your location and room layout.*

## 5. Skipping Key Features for a Lower Price

**Mistake:** Sacrificing essential features like **auto-clean**, **dehumidifier**, or **PM 2.5 filters** to save a few thousand rupees.

**Why it's wrong:** These features impact air quality, convenience, and AC life. For instance, in polluted cities, a basic dust filter won’t be enough.

💡 *Balance cost with feature importance based on your needs.*

## 6. Neglecting Noise Levels

**Mistake:** Buying an AC without checking its noise levels.

**Why it's wrong:** Some budget or window ACs are quite noisy, especially during the night. Look for decibel (dB) ratings in the specifications.

😴 *Choose models with < 40 dB for bedroom use.*

## 7. Ignoring Installation and After-Sales Service

**Mistake:** Going for the cheapest online deal without checking **installation quality** or **local service availability**.

**Why it's wrong:** Poor installation can reduce cooling efficiency and even void warranties. Some brands have limited service in smaller towns.

🛠️ *Prefer brands with a strong service network and verified installation partners.*

## 8. Not Comparing Prices Online

**Mistake:** Buying directly from a local store without checking deals online.

**Why it's wrong:** You might miss out on discounts, combo offers, or cashback available on platforms like Amazon, Flipkart, and Croma.

💰 *Compare online and offline prices — and don’t forget extended warranty offers.*

## 9. Assuming All Brands Are the Same

**Mistake:** Picking a brand only because it's popular.

**Why it's wrong:** Every brand has its strengths. Some are better at energy efficiency (like Daikin or Hitachi), while others offer great features (like LG or Panasonic).

🔍 *Read reviews, compare features, and check service feedback before finalizing.*

## Final Thoughts

Buying an AC is a long-term investment, especially in a country like India where summers are getting harsher each year. Avoiding these common mistakes will help you choose the right air conditioner that keeps you cool, saves electricity, and offers value for money.
