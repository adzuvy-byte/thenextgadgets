## What to check

- **Exact model.** Protectors are model-specific. The iPhone 17 and 17 Pro share a size but check the listing.
- **Alignment frame.** Makes installation almost foolproof.
- **Case friendly.** Edge-to-edge glass can lift if the case presses on it.
- **Privacy or clear.** Privacy glass narrows the viewing angle.
- **Installation.** Clean the screen, remove dust and do it indoors.
