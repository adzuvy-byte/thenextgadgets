## How to choose a dishwasher

- **Capacity:** Capacity is measured in place settings. The four models here hold 14 or 15 place settings, which suits larger families or frequent guests. Smaller households can look at 12 place models.
- **Cleaning performance:** Look for an intensive program for greasy Indian cookware, plus eco, glass and quick options.
- **Energy and water use:** Compare the listed kWh per cycle and litres per wash. Sensors that adjust water to the load help.
- **Noise:** Check the decibel figure if the kitchen is open to a living area.
- **Racks and features:** Adjustable racks, cutlery trays, delay start and half-load options add everyday convenience. A stainless steel tub is built to last.
- **Brand and service:** Check warranty terms and service coverage in your city before buying.
- **Budget:** Prices change often, so check the current price on the listing.
