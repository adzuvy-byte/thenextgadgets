## How to choose

- **Check the exam rules.** Many boards allow non-programmable models only.
- **Natural display.** Shows fractions and roots as written. The 991ES Plus and Classwiz have it.
- **Functions.** The 991 series has more than the 82 series.
- **Solar and battery.** Most use both.
- **Genuine product.** Check the seller to avoid fakes.
