## How to choose

- **Resolution.** On a 32-inch screen, HD is usually fine from a normal distance. Full HD is a small step up.
- **Smart platform.** Android or Google TV gives the most apps. webOS and Tizen are smooth but have different app lists.
- **Sound.** Small TVs have small speakers. Plan a soundbar if you care.
- **Ports.** Check HDMI and USB counts.
- **Brand and service.** Samsung and LG cost more but have wider service.
