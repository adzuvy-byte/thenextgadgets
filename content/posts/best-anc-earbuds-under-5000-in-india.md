## How to choose ANC earbuds

- **ANC figure.** dB numbers on listings (50, 52, 55) are the maximum the maker claims. They are not directly comparable between brands, but higher usually means stronger cancelling.
- **Battery.** The realme Buds Air 8 lists 58 hours, the Oppo and OnePlus Nord Buds 4 list 54, and the Samsung lists 35. These are totals with the case.
- **Codec.** The realme, Oppo and OnePlus listings mention LHDC or Hi-Res audio, which only helps if your phone supports it.
- **Water resistance.** The two realme models list IP55. The Samsung lists IP54.
- **Phone match.** The Samsung's Galaxy AI features and the OnePlus's extras tend to work best with the same brand's phones.
- **Price.** Some models may be above ₹5,000 on a given day. Check the live price.
