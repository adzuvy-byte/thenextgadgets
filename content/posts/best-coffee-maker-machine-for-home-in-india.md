## Drip or espresso?

- **Drip makers** brew a jug of filter-style coffee with little effort. Cheapest, and good for a family.
- **Espresso machines** make a concentrated shot with steam for milk. Better for cappuccino and latte, but they need practice and cleaning.

## What to check

- **Capacity.** A 4-cup maker is for one or two people. 6 cups serves a family.
- **Power.** Higher watts brew faster.
- **Carafe material.** Glass is easy to clean, but it breaks.
- **Descaling.** Hard water scales the heater. Descale monthly.
- **Filters.** Check you can buy paper or reusable filters.
