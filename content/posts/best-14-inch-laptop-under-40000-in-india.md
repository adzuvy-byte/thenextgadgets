## How to choose

- **Windows or Chromebook.** Chromebooks are quick for browsing and school work, but they run ChromeOS, not Windows software.
- **Celeron N4500 and N4020.** Fine for browsing, documents and video calls, but slow for multitasking.
- **Ryzen 3 7320U.** The fastest chip in this group.
- **RAM.** Prefer 8GB or more. 4GB feels tight.

## What to check

- **Operating system.** Confirm Windows, ChromeOS or PrimeOS on the listing.
- **Display.** FHD looks sharper than HD.
- **Storage type.** SSD or UFS is faster than eMMC.
- **Warranty.** Check service coverage in your city.
