## Countertop or portable?

- **Countertop** (NutriPro, Pigeon) are mixer-grinders that also make smoothies. Stronger, handle ice and fibrous fruit and fit family use.
- **Portable** blenders are battery-powered bottles for one serving. Light and handy for the gym or office, but weaker.

## What to check

- **Power.** Watts matter for ice and frozen fruit.
- **Battery.** Portables need charging. Check run time.
- **Cleaning.** Rinse straight after use.
- **Capacity.** Portables are 400 to 550ml, enough for one serving.
