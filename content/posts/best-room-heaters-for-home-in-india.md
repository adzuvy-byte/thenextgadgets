## How to choose a room heater

- **Match the wattage to the room.** A rough rule of thumb is about 10 watts per square foot in a normally insulated room. A 1500 to 2000 W heater suits a room of about 150 to 200 sq ft. An 800 W heater is for spot heating.
- **Pick the heater type.** Quartz, carbon and halogen heat you directly and quickly. PTC ceramic fan heaters warm the air and spread it with a fan. Oil-filled radiators heat slowly and keep warm longest.
- **Check the safety features.** Look for overheat protection and a tip-over switch. A thermostat helps the heater cycle instead of running flat out.
- **Check your wiring.** A 2000 W heater draws about 8.7 A at 230 V. Plug it into a wall socket rated for it, not a light extension board.
- **Estimate running cost.** Cost per hour is the heater's kilowatts times your electricity rate per unit. A 2000 W heater uses 2 units per hour at full power.
- **Warranty.** Most heaters come with one to two years of warranty. Check the listing for the exact term.

## Using a room heater safely

Keep the heater at least a metre from curtains, bedding and furniture. Never dry clothes on it or cover it. Do not leave it running when you are out, and unplug it when you leave the room unless the manual says otherwise. Electric heaters burn no fuel, so they do not produce carbon monoxide, but they can make a closed room feel dry. A small ventilation gap and a bowl of water do no harm.
