## Philips Air Purifier: Why CADR Matters

One of the biggest mistakes buyers make is choosing an air purifier based only on the phrase "covers up to X square feet."

**CADR is a much more useful specification to compare.**

CADR stands for Clean Air Delivery Rate. It tells you how quickly a purifier can deliver filtered air.

For example, the AC1711 and AC1715 both have a CADR of 300 m³/h, while the AC2936 is rated at 380 m³/h and the AC3059 at 520 m³/h.

This is why you shouldn't automatically buy a larger-looking purifier just because the manufacturer claims a bigger coverage area.

Room size, ceiling height, doors and windows, outdoor pollution, occupancy and how long the purifier runs all affect real-world results.

## Is Philips AC1711 Better Than AC1715?

Not necessarily.

The AC1711 and AC1715 are extremely similar in their core purification specifications. Both are rated at 300 m³/h and cover rooms up to 36 m². Both use HEPA and activated-carbon filtration.

The major difference is connectivity.

The AC1715 connects to Philips' Air+ app, while the AC1711 is the simpler option.

That makes the AC1711 the better **value choice**, while the AC1715 is the better choice if you specifically want smart controls.

A third-party test of the AC1715 also found that its filtration performance was broadly competitive in its price segment, although performance varied compared with competing models and testing conditions.

## Are Philips Air Purifiers Good for Indian Homes?

They can be a sensible choice, particularly in areas where particulate pollution is a recurring problem.

But an air purifier cannot clean an entire home simply because the manufacturer lists a large theoretical coverage area.

For the best results:

- Keep doors and windows closed when outdoor pollution is high.
- Choose a purifier with enough CADR for the room.
- Keep the air inlet and outlet unobstructed.
- Use Auto or Turbo mode when pollution levels rise.
- Run the purifier consistently rather than only for a few minutes.
- Replace the filter when required.
- Clean the exterior and pre-filter according to the manufacturer's instructions.

## What About Philips' HEPA Filtration?

Philips uses multi-stage filtration on several of its current models, including HEPA and activated-carbon filters. The HEPA layer is intended to capture fine particles, while activated carbon is useful for certain gases and odors.

However, the presence of a HEPA filter alone doesn't determine how effective an air purifier will be in your room.

The overall system matters:

**Filter + CADR + airflow + room size + operating time + placement**

That is why two air purifiers with similarly rated filters can perform differently in actual use.

## Philips Air Purifier Placement Matters

Where you put the purifier can have a noticeable effect on how quickly it cleans the room.

Avoid placing it:

- Behind furniture
- Directly against a wall when the design requires clearance
- Inside a cabinet
- Immediately next to curtains
- In a location where its air outlet is blocked

For bedrooms, placing the purifier where air can circulate freely is generally more useful than hiding it in a corner.

A third-party test of the AC1715 found slightly different PM2.5 results depending on whether the purifier was positioned in a corner or nearer the centre of the room, illustrating why placement can matter.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which is the best Philips air purifier?

For most medium-sized rooms, the Philips AC1711 is a strong value choice because it combines a 300 m³/h CADR with HEPA and activated-carbon filtration. The AC1715 is a better choice if you want Air+ app connectivity.

### Is Philips AC1711 better than AC1715?

The AC1711 is better value if you don't need smart connectivity. The AC1715 offers similar core purification specifications but adds Air+ app connectivity.

### Which Philips air purifier is best for a bedroom?

For a smaller bedroom, the AC0950 can be appropriate. For a medium-sized bedroom, the AC1711 or AC1715 provides a higher 300 m³/h CADR and is rated for rooms up to 36 m².

### How often should a Philips air purifier filter be replaced?

Filter life depends on the model, usage and pollution levels. Rather than relying on a generic replacement interval, follow the filter-life indicator and the maintenance instructions for your exact Philips model.

### Is a higher CADR always better?

Not necessarily. Higher CADR is useful for larger rooms and faster air cleaning, but buying a much larger purifier than necessary can increase your upfront cost without providing proportional benefits. Room size and operating conditions should be considered together with CADR.

### Does Philips air purifier remove PM2.5?

Philips air purifiers with HEPA filtration are designed to capture fine particulate matter, including PM2.5. The actual reduction in a room depends on the purifier's CADR, room conditions, placement and operating time.
