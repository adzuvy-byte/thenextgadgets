The MacBook Air and the Dell XPS are the two thin-and-light laptops people most often cross-shop. The current Apple model is the 13-inch MacBook Air with the M5 chip, and Dell has refreshed the XPS family for 2026, including a new XPS 13 and a larger XPS 14. This guide compares them on the things that decide the choice: display, battery, ports, memory and price. Figures come from Smartprix's long-term review of the M5 MacBook Air and RTINGS's XPS 14 vs MacBook Air comparison, and Dell's India launch coverage. We do not claim exact Indian prices for every XPS configuration, because they vary by processor and RAM. Check the live price before you decide.

## The short answer

- **Pick the MacBook Air M5** for battery life, silent fanless design, consistent performance and a long software life.
- **Pick a Dell XPS** if you want Windows, an OLED screen, more ports or a touchscreen, or need more RAM options.

## Price in India

Apple raised Indian MacBook Air prices on June 25, 2026. The 13-inch M5 (16 GB, 512 GB) is now ₹1,49,990, with education pricing around ₹1,37,900. The launch price was ₹1,19,900, and the Smartprix reviewer bought one for about ₹1 lakh during a sale. Dell launched a new India lineup in 2026 with prices starting at ₹79,990, anchored by a roughly 1 kg XPS 13. XPS prices rise a lot with the processor, screen and RAM you choose, so compare the exact configurations, not the starting price.

## Display

The XPS 14's 2.8K OLED panel at 120 Hz has deeper blacks and smoother motion, and it is the better screen for movies and general viewing. The MacBook Air's IPS panel is more colour accurate out of the box and fine for work, but it is 60 Hz and hard to read in direct sunlight.

## Battery and performance

The MacBook Air wins battery life. Smartprix measured about 12 hours of screen-on time on average, 10.5 to 11 hours on heavy days and 13 to 14 on light days. Because it is fanless, it also holds performance steadily over long workloads, according to RTINGS. Neither laptop is meant for heavy GPU work.

## Ports, keyboard and everyday use

The Dell offers a third USB-C port and a 4K webcam. The MacBook has MagSafe charging, a headphone jack and a keyboard and trackpad that the reviewer still rates highly after months of use. Downsides of the MacBook are the notch, only 512 GB of storage at this price, no microSD slot and both USB-C ports on one side. The bundled charger is only 30 W, so a faster one costs extra.

## Who should buy which

| If you... | Buy |
|---|---|
| Want the longest battery and a silent laptop | MacBook Air M5 |
| Use Windows-only software | Dell XPS |
| Want an OLED touchscreen | Dell XPS 14 |
| Want 32 GB or more RAM | Dell XPS |
| Use an iPhone and want a seamless ecosystem | MacBook Air M5 |
| Want the lowest price | A cheaper XPS configuration, or an older MacBook Air |
