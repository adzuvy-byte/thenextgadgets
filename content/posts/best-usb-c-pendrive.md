## How to choose

- **Connector.** Type-C only for phones and new laptops. Dual (Type-A + Type-C) works everywhere.
- **Speed.** Look for USB 3.0 or above and the read speed in MB/s.
- **Capacity.** 64GB is a fair start.
- **Warranty.** SanDisk drives list 5 years.
- **Build.** Metal drives resist wear better.
