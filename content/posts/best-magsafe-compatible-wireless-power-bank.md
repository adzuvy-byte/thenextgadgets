## What to know

- **Wireless is slower than a cable.** 15W wireless still takes longer than USB-C. Use the cable when you are in a hurry.
- **Fit.** The magnet works best on a MagSafe-compatible phone or a MagSafe case. A thick case can weaken it.
- **Heat.** Wireless charging runs warm. Do not cover the phone and power bank in a pocket for long.
- **Capacity.** 10,000mAh gives about 1.5 phone charges after losses.
- **Built-in cable and stand.** Some models add a kickstand or cable. Check the listing.
