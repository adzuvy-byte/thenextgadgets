When you shop for an oil-filled radiator, the number of fins is the first thing the listing tells you, and it is the easiest way to narrow the field. More fins give the radiator more surface to release heat, so a 13-fin model warms a bigger room than a 9-fin model at a similar wattage. This page sorts six radiators by fin count: 13 fins, 11 fins and 9 fins. Details come from the listing titles, so check wattage, thermostat and warranty on the current page before you buy.

## How many fins do you need?

As a rough guide, 13 fins suit a medium to large bedroom, 11 fins a medium bedroom, and 9 fins a small room. Insulation, ceiling height and the local climate change the answer, so treat this as a starting point. ## Safety basics

- A 2900 W radiator draws about 12.6 A. Plug it into a wall socket rated for the load, not a light extension board.
- Keep it upright and away from curtains and bedding.
- Do not dry clothes on it.
- Do not leave any heater unattended unless the manual explicitly allows it.
