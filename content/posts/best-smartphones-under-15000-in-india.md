## About each phone

Prices and ratings were checked on Amazon.in on 2026-11-09 and change often. The descriptions follow what each listing states. We have not benchmarked these phones ourselves, so treat processor and speed claims as the brand's own figures.

### Samsung Galaxy M07 (₹11,999)

The best-rated and most reassuring pick, at 4.1 from 2,919 buyers. It uses a MediaTek Helio G99 processor, has 4GB RAM and 64GB storage, and a 6.7-inch HD+ display at 90Hz. The 5,000mAh battery charges at 25W. The camera is 50MP at the back and 8MP at the front. It has IP54 dust and splash protection, and Samsung promises 6 years of Android upgrades and security updates on the listing, which is unusually long at this price. It is a 4G phone.

Who it suits: buyers who plan to keep a phone for years and want clean software and long update support. The trade-off is a modest HD+ screen and no 5G.

### realme Narzo 80 Lite 4G (₹11,998)

Built around battery. The listing gives a 6,300mAh cell in a 7.94 mm body, IP54 protection and a customisable Pulse Light on the back for notifications. It has 4GB RAM and 64GB storage, and is rated 4.1 from 2,367 buyers. It is a 4G phone, so skip it if you want 5G.

Who it suits: people who care most about getting through two days on one charge. It costs about the same as the Samsung, so the choice is long battery (realme) against long update support (Samsung).

### realme C71 4G (₹13,499)

The most reviewed phone here, with 16,933 ratings at 4.2. It has a 6.75-inch 90Hz screen, a 6,300mAh battery with 15W charging, 37MP cameras and IP54 protection. The charging speed is the slowest on this list, so a full charge takes a long time. It is also a 4G phone.

Who it suits: buyers who want a big screen and battery from a brand with a large rating base. At ₹13,499 it costs more than the Narzo 80 Lite, so ask whether the larger display is worth the difference.

### Motorola g37 5G (₹14,799)

The only phone here with a clear, current 5G chipset on the listing: a MediaTek Dimensity 6400 with 4GB LPDDR4X RAM and 64GB UFS 2.2 storage. Storage can be expanded up to 1TB with a microSD card. It is rated 3.9, but from only 45 buyers, so the score is not yet reliable.

Who it suits: buyers who want 5G and expandable storage. Read recent reviews before buying, because the early rating base is small.

### REDMI A7 Pro 5G (₹14,489)

The listing promises a 6,300mAh battery, 15W charging with 7.5W reverse charging, a 32MP dual camera and HyperOS 3 with Google Gemini and Circle to Search. It is rated 3.4 from 3,270 buyers, the lowest of the six. "Segment's fastest processor" is the brand's claim, so treat it with caution.

Who it suits: buyers who want a Redmi with 5G and a large battery. Check the negative reviews first. If the rating worries you, the Motorola costs about the same.

### POCO C85x 5G (₹13,749)

A 5G phone with a 6.9-inch display, a 32MP dual AI camera, 6,300mAh battery, 4GB RAM and 64GB storage. It is rated 3.5 from 439 buyers. The listing gives little detail on the processor, which makes it harder to judge performance.

Who it suits: buyers who want the biggest screen and 5G. Because the processor is not named on the listing, confirm the chipset before you buy.

## How to choose

- **5G or 4G.** 5G is future-proof, but only if your area has coverage.
- **RAM and storage.** 4GB and 64GB is basic. Check the card slot for more space.
- **Battery.** 6000mAh or more gives two days for many users.
- **Software updates.** Samsung and Motorola tend to offer cleaner software.
- **Camera.** At this price, rear cameras are basic.
- **Price.** These prices move daily, and sales and bank offers can shave more off.

## What to know about phones under ₹15,000

- **4GB and 64GB will feel tight.** Android and a few apps use a large share of 64GB. Plan on a microSD card where the phone has a slot, and clear app caches regularly.
- **A big battery is not always a long-lasting one.** The 6,300mAh phones here charge at only 15W, so a full charge takes a while. Make sure you are comfortable with overnight charging.
- **5G only helps where you have coverage.** Check your network's 5G in your area and whether the phone supports the bands your operator uses before paying for it.
- **Ignore processor marketing.** Phrases like "segment's fastest" come from the brand. At this price, watch for slow app opening and lag in reviews instead.
- **Software updates matter.** A phone that gets security patches for years is worth more than a slightly better spec sheet. Samsung states 6 years on its listing. Check the others.
- **Buy from a seller you trust.** Prices move daily, and bank offers can cut the effective price. Check the live offer before you decide.

## Should you spend a little more?

If you can stretch the budget by a few thousand rupees, 6GB or 8GB of RAM and 128GB of storage make a bigger difference to daily use than any other upgrade. Under ₹15,000, the phones above are good for calls, messaging, video and light games.
