- **5G or 4G.** 5G is future-proof, but only if your area has coverage.
- **RAM and storage.** 4GB and 64GB is basic. Check the card slot for more space.
- **Battery.** 6000mAh or more gives two days for many users.
- **Software updates.** Samsung and Motorola tend to offer cleaner software.
- **Camera.** At this price, rear cameras are basic.
- **Price.** These prices move daily, and sales and bank offers can shave more off.
