## Why Proper Installation Matters

Installing your 70mai dash cam correctly ensures:

- **Clear video recording** without obstruction
- **Stable power connection** to avoid sudden shutdowns
- **Accurate parking monitoring**
- **Long-lasting device performance**

Improper installation can lead to blurred footage, frequent disconnections, or sensor errors.

## Step-by-Step 70mai Installation in India

### Step 1: Choose the Right Mounting Spot

- Most 70mai cameras are **mounted on the windshield**, behind the rearview mirror.
- Ensure the camera has a **clear view of the road** and doesn’t obstruct the driver’s vision.
- Clean the glass with a dry cloth before sticking the mount for a **firm hold**.

### Step 2: Attach the Dash Cam to the Mount

- Most 70mai models come with a **3M adhesive mount or suction cup**.
- Press the mount firmly on the windshield and let it set for a few seconds.
- Slide the camera onto the mount and **adjust the angle** for optimal road coverage.

### Step 3: Connect Power

- Use the **included 12V car adapter** or a **hardwiring kit** for constant power.
- Run the cable along the **headliner, A-pillar, and dashboard** to keep it neat.
- For a permanent setup, consider professional **hardwiring to the fuse box** for parking mode.

### Step 4: Insert a MicroSD Card

- Most 70mai cameras require a **MicroSD card** (Class 10 recommended).
- Insert the card and format it in-camera if prompted.
- Ensure sufficient storage for high-quality recordings.

### Step 5: Connect to the 70mai App

- Download the **70mai app** from **Google Play Store or Apple App Store**.
- Turn on **Wi-Fi on the dash cam**.
- Open the app, **connect the device**, and follow the on-screen instructions.
- You can now **view live footage, change settings, and download videos** to your phone.

### Step 6: Test Your Dash Cam

- Start your car and check the recording status.
- Adjust the camera angle if necessary.
- Make sure features like **G-sensor, loop recording, and parking monitoring** are active.

## Tips for 70mai Installation in India

1. **Avoid direct sunlight:** Mount the camera away from intense glare for clearer videos.
2. **Keep cables hidden:** Tuck wires into panels for a clean look.
3. **Use original accessories:** Only use 70mai cables and mounts to prevent damage.
4. **Check local regulations:** Ensure dash cam placement does not obstruct your field of vision.
5. **Regular maintenance:** Clean the lens periodically for crystal-clear footage.

## Benefits of Proper Installation

- **Accident evidence:** High-quality recordings can be crucial for insurance claims.
- **Parking security:** Protects your vehicle while parked with motion detection.
- **Convenience:** Easy access to videos on your smartphone via the app.
- **Enhanced driving safety:** Helps monitor traffic and driving conditions.
