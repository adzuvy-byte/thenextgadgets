## Similarities (what they share)

- **Filtration & performance**: Both use a 3-layer filtration system (pre-filter + active carbon + NanoProtect HEPA) and claim to remove 99.97% of particles down to 0.003 microns.
- **CADR & Coverage**: Both have a Clean Air Delivery Rate (CADR) of ~300 m³/hr and are rated for rooms up to ~36 m² (≈ 380 sq.ft), suitable for bedrooms/medium rooms.
- **Noise & Power**: Sleep-mode noise can drop to ~15 dB; in Turbo mode max ~50 dB. Max power consumption ~27 W.
- **Physical build**: Roughly same size/weight (about 3.78 kg; dimensions ~273 × 486 × 273 mm) which means portability and placement flexibility are similar.

## Key Differences

| Feature / Aspect | AC1711/63 | AC1715/60 |
|---|---|---|
| **Smart features** | Basic — no Wi-Fi, no app/voice control (manual controls + auto mode) | Smart — supports Wi-Fi, app control, voice control (Alexa / Google Home) via the “Air+” app |
| **Sensor & automation** | Has air quality sensor and auto mode, but fewer “smart” comforts — mainly basic auto & manual speed settings. | More advanced: has professional-grade sensor (AeraSense), real-time air quality detection (PM2.5 and more), automatic speed adjustment, smart AQI display & ambient-light/night mode. |
| **User convenience** | Easier maintenance but more “manual”; good for users who just want plug-and-play, no frills. | More convenience: remote/app/voice control, auto adjustments — better for tech-savvy or smart homes. |
| **Price / Value positioning** | Usually a bit cheaper than AC1715/60. | Slightly higher price — but offers smart features and extra convenience. |

## Which One Should You Buy — Based on Use Case

- **Go for AC1711/63** if: You just need a **reliable air purifier for a bedroom or small living room**. You don’t care about smart features or remote control. You want a **budget-friendly, simple, low-maintenance purifier**.
- **Go for AC1715/60** if: You want **smart home integration** — Wi-Fi, app control, voice commands. You prefer **automatic air quality monitoring** and adjustment without manual intervention. You want the **latest features and convenience**, especially if the purifier will run often or in multiple rooms.

## My Verdict (For Most Users in Indian Cities like Gurgaon / Delhi–NCR)

If your main goal is to **deal with pollution, dust, allergens** in a **bedroom or small/medium room**, and you don’t need app/voice control — **AC1711/63** gives you **Best “bang for buck.”**

But if you prefer **automation, convenience and smart-home compatibility** (for example, you might turn it on/off remotely, want real-time AQI monitoring, or integrate with smart home devices) — **AC1715/60** is worth the extra money.
