## Which wattage do you need?

- **Up to 60 W:** many ultrabooks and tablets. A basic cable is enough, and our general [USB-C cable guide](/best-usb-c-cable-in-india/) covers those.
- **61 to 100 W:** most 14-inch and 15-inch laptops. A 100 W cable is the safe pick.
- **Above 100 W:** only if your laptop and charger both support it. A 240 W cable does nothing for a 65 W laptop.

## What to check on the listing

- **Charger and device.** The cable, the charger and the device each cap the speed. The slowest of the three wins.
- **E-marker chip.** Cables above 60 W carry a chip that tells the charger what current is safe. A genuine 100 W or 240 W cable has one.
- **Data speed.** Some charging cables carry data only at USB 2.0 speed (480 Mbps). If you move files or connect a display, check the listed data rate separately.
- **Length.** The lengths here are 1.5 m or 2 m, which is handy at a desk but can drop a little power over distance.
- **Certification.** Look for USB-IF or Apple certification on the listing, and read the warranty.
