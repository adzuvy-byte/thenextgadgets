The iPhone 16e unexpectedly surpasses the more expensive iPhone 16 in one crucial aspect—battery life. While the iPhone 16 comes packed with premium features, the 16e manages to outshine it in terms of endurance, making it an excellent choice for users who need a device that lasts longer on a single charge.

One of the key reasons behind this improvement is the inclusion of a more power-efficient modem. Apple has introduced a new C1 modem in the iPhone 16e, designed to reduce power consumption while maintaining strong connectivity and performance. As a result, the iPhone 16e can achieve up to 26 hours of video playback, compared to the iPhone 16’s 22 hours. This translates to nearly 20% longer battery life, which can make a significant difference for those who rely heavily on their phones throughout the day.

Per Apple’s website, here’s the expected battery life for each model:

- **iPhone 16e**: Up to ***26 hours*** video playback
- **iPhone 16**: Up to ***22 hours*** video playback

Apple's Tech Specs for the iPhone 16e states:

Video Playback: Up to 26 hours

Video Playback (Streamed): Up to 21 hours

Those numbers *are* better than the iPhone 16's 22 hours / 18 hours (streamed) ratings, but that could also be explained by the iPhone 16e's lower brightness (800 typical, 1200 peak) compared to the iPhone 16 (1000 typical, 1600-2000 peak), or it may be that the iPhone 16e is underclocked and able to last longer because the CPU is just doing less. It's likely a combination of all three - modem, screen brightness, and CPU power draw.

Battery life is one of the most critical factors for smartphone users, especially for professionals, travelers, and gamers who require extended usage without frequent charging. While Apple typically reserves its Best battery optimizations for its Pro models, the iPhone 16e’s efficiency gives it an edge over its pricier sibling.

Despite being a more affordable option in the iPhone 16 lineup, the 16e's impressive battery performance makes it an attractive choice for users looking for longevity without spending extra on higher-end models. Whether for work, entertainment, or daily tasks, the iPhone 16e ensures that users can go longer between charges, making it a practical and cost-effective option.
