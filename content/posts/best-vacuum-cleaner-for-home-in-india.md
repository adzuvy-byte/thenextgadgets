## Product-by-product notes

**AGARO Ace 1600 W wet-dry.** A 21-litre tank, 21.5 kPa suction and a blower function, rated 4.3 by about 7,700 buyers at around ₹5,799. It handles dust, spills and car cleaning. It is large and heavy, so it suits a store room better than a small flat.

**Philips PowerPro FC9352.** A compact bagless vacuum with a 1900 W motor and PowerCyclone 5, a design that separates dust from the air stream. It is dry-only, and it has over 23,000 ratings at 4.3. Choose it for daily floor cleaning and a known brand with service.

**SEZNIK 3-in-1.** A corded handheld that vacuums dry dirt, picks up wet spills and mops, with suction of over 20 kPa. It is rated 4.4 by about 5,100 buyers at around ₹4,298. The brand is newer, so check recent reviews on durability. A small device for a small home, not a replacement for a full-size vacuum.

**Inalsa 10 L wet-dry.** A 1200 W, 17 kPa machine with a blower and a safety float that stops suction when the tank is full of liquid. Around ₹3,701 and rated 4.0 by about 11,000 buyers. A budget wet-dry for a small home.

**Eureka Forbes Cyclo Vac.** A bagless cyclonic vacuum with 24 kPa suction, a 2.5 L tank and six accessories. It is dry-only. Around ₹7,199 and rated 4.2 by about 6,600 buyers. A good fit for homes with tile and marble floors and mixed dust.

**AGARO Regal 800 W handheld.** A light, small 6.5 kPa vacuum with a 0.8 L dust container, at around ₹1,430. It cleans sofas, car seats and corners, but it is not a floor vacuum. About 20,900 buyers have rated it 4.0.

**BLACK+DECKER WDBD15.** A 15-litre wet-dry vacuum with a washable HEPA filter and a 16 kPa motor. Around ₹7,197 and rated 4.0 by about 3,700 buyers. Choose it over the AGARO Ace if the HEPA filter matters to you, for example with dust allergies, and accept lower suction.

**ECOVACS DEEBOT Y1 PRO.** A robot that vacuums and mops together and senses carpets, so it avoids them when mopping. It runs up to 320 minutes on a charge. At around ₹19,999 it costs several times a corded vacuum, and it handles daily upkeep, not heavy cleaning. Choose it if you want hands-free floor care.


## How to choose

- **Wet-dry or dry only.** Wet-dry machines also handle spills and can double for car cleaning.
- **Suction (kPa).** Higher kPa means stronger pickup.
- **Bagless or bagged.** Bagless saves money on bags but needs filter cleaning.
- **Tank size.** 10L is fine for small homes. 20L+ for bigger spaces.
- **Noise and weight.** Check reviews on both.
