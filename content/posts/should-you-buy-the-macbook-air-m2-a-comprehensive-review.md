The MacBook Air with the M2 chip came out in June 2022. It is no longer Apple's newest Air, but it is still sold second-hand and refurbished, and often discounted in India. This guide explains who should still buy it in 2026 and who should spend more. Points here are based on BGR's 2026 assessment of the M2 MacBook Air, and India prices come from sale reports. Prices move often, so check live listings.

## The short answer

Yes, the M2 MacBook Air is still a solid buy for light work, if you get it at a good price. It is a good laptop for students, browsing, documents, spreadsheets, video calls and light photo editing. It is not the right choice if you need lots of RAM or heavy editing.

## Specs worth knowing

- **Display:** 13.6-inch screen.
- **Camera:** 1080p FaceTime HD webcam.
- **Ports:** two Thunderbolt USB-C ports, a MagSafe charging port and a 3.5 mm headphone jack.
- **Wireless:** Wi-Fi 6 and Bluetooth 5.3.
- **Battery:** Apple claims up to 15 hours of wireless web browsing.
- **External display:** up to 6K at 60 Hz.
- **Memory:** it launched with 8 GB of RAM. Used models can have up to 24 GB and up to 2 TB of storage.

## What is good

- Fast enough for everyday work, and fanless and silent.
- A more complete design than the M1 Air, with MagSafe and a better webcam.
- Software support should last. Apple has not signalled an early cutoff for Apple Silicon Macs, and BGR expects updates for at least about four more years, though that is an estimate.

## What holds it back

- **8 GB of RAM** is the main limit. If you keep many apps and tabs open, it will feel tight.
- Newer Apple Intelligence features could favour Macs with more RAM, though that is speculation.
- Not ideal for heavy local AI work, serious video editing or demanding 3D graphics. A MacBook Pro, which has fans for sustained performance, is better for that.

## Price in India

Sale reports in early 2026 showed the M2 Air at roughly ₹61,000 to ₹65,000 on Flipkart's Republic Day sale. Those were sale prices, not permanent ones. Check live prices on Amazon.in, and make sure you are buying a new or properly refurbished unit with a valid warranty. For comparison, a new M5 Air costs ₹1,49,990 after Apple's June 2026 price rise.

## Should you buy it, upgrade or choose the M1?

| If you... | Choose |
|---|---|
| Want the best value for light work | MacBook Air M2 on sale |
| Want to spend even less | [MacBook Air M1](/why-the-macbook-air-m1-is-still-a-brilliant-buy/), preferably refurbished with 16 GB |
| Need more RAM, storage or displays | A newer M4 or M5 Air |
| Do heavy editing or 3D work | A MacBook Pro |

## Buying tips

- Prefer 16 GB RAM if you can find it. 8 GB is acceptable for light use.
- Check battery health on used or refurbished units.
- Buy from a seller who gives a warranty and a proper invoice.
- Compare against the [M5 MacBook Air vs Dell XPS](/macbook-air-vs-dell-xps-which-laptop-should-you-buy/) if you are open to Windows.
