## Hard water: what to know

Titles do not say how a washer handles hard water, so we do not claim any model is better. In hard water areas:

- Use a **water softener** or a detergent made for hard water.
- Run a **descaling cycle** with citric acid every few months.
- Wash with **more detergent** if clothes feel stiff, and **rinse well**.
- **Stainless steel tubs** resist scale better than plastic.

## How to choose

- **Capacity.** 7.5 to 8.5 kg suits most families. 9.5 kg is for bigger loads.
- **Star rating.** 5 stars save electricity.
- **Pulsator.** Roller or wave pulsators clean differently. Check reviews.
- **Dryer.** Air Turbo or similar spin dryers cut drying time.
