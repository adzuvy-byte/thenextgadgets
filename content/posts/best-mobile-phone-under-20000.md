## Which one fits you?

If battery is the priority, the Motorola Moto g77 Power and the OnePlus N6x both list 7000 mAh. The Samsung phones list 6000 mAh. If you want the broadest feature list, the Galaxy M17e adds IP54, Knox security and a long OS upgrade promise. If the budget is tight, the Galaxy A07 is the most basic of the five.

## How to choose a phone at this price

- **Battery.** 6000 mAh and above is common here. Bigger cells last longer but add weight.
- **Display.** 120 Hz makes scrolling smoother. FHD+ is sharper than HD+.
- **RAM and storage.** 6GB RAM with 128GB storage is a comfortable base. 4GB RAM or 64GB storage can feel limiting after a year.
- **Software support.** A longer update promise matters more than a slightly faster chip.
- **Check the listing.** Colour, RAM and storage variants often have different prices.
