## How to choose

- **ANC or not.** ANC cuts outside noise but adds to price. Neckbands without it are cheaper with longer battery.
- **Battery.** 40 to 50 hours is common.
- **Fit.** A neckband is secure for runs and workouts.
- **Calls.** ENC mics help on calls in noisy places.
- **Charging.** Fast charge helps a lot.
