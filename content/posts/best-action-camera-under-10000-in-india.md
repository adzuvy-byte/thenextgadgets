## About each action camera

Prices and ratings below were checked on Amazon.in on 2026-11-09 and change often. The descriptions come from what each listing states. We have not run these cameras in a lab, so treat claims such as "5K" or "48MP" as the seller's numbers, not our test results.

### AKASO EK7000 4K Action Camera (₹7,999)

This is the best rated camera here, at 4.5 from 263 buyers, and the safest starting point for most people. It records 4K at 30 fps or 2.7K at 30 fps, takes 20MP photos, and uses electronic image stabilisation (EIS) to smooth shake. It comes with a waterproof housing rated to about 40 m and a 2.4G wrist remote, which is handy when the camera is mounted on a helmet or handlebar. The listing says the remote itself is not waterproof. Wi-Fi works with the AKASO GO app within about 10 m, and there is HDMI output for a TV.

Who it suits: first-time buyers, bike and trip clips, and anyone who wants a remote included. The trade-off is the small 2-inch screen and 30 fps ceiling, which is normal at this price.

### AKASO EK7000 4K30FPS 20MP WiFi (₹6,398)

A cheaper listing of the same EK7000 family, rated 4.2 from 295 buyers. The stated video, 20MP photos, EIS and 4X zoom match the first AKASO, and it adds support for an external microphone. The waterproof case is listed at 131 ft, which is the same depth in feet, and a microSD card is not included. Check the box contents on the live listing before you decide, because the two AKASO listings differ in accessories and price.

Who it suits: buyers who want the AKASO brand and ratings for about ₹1,600 less. If the accessory list is shorter than on the pricier listing, the saving may disappear once you buy a mount.

### SJCAM C110 Plus (₹9,199)

A small, light pocket camera. The listing gives 79 g, 4K at 30 fps with a 170° ultra-wide view, an F1.8 aperture, 20MP photos and 6-axis gyro stabilisation. It is waterproof to about 5 m on its own and 40 m with the supplied case, and it has a 1100mAh battery that the listing rates at up to about 2.1 hours of recording. The brand is well known among action camera buyers, but only 11 people have rated this listing, so the 4.0 average is not yet reliable.

Who it suits: travellers and vloggers who want something thumb-sized. It is the most expensive pick here and has the shortest stated battery life, so carry a spare.

### FitSpark Eagle i15 Pro Max Creator Pack (₹8,549)

Sold as a bundle. The listing claims real 4K at 30 fps with a Sony STARVIS sensor, EIS, a 2-inch touchscreen plus a 1.4-inch selfie screen, an IP68 case to 30 m and two 1000mAh batteries for 240 to 300 minutes of recording. A wired Type-C microphone is included, and it supports only the original FitSpark mic. It is rated 4.0 from 57 buyers.

Who it suits: riders and creators who want the dual screens, a mic and a second battery in one box. The brand is newer than AKASO or SJCAM, so read recent reviews on warranty and support before buying.

### Digitek DAC-002 5K (₹6,999)

The cheapest 5K label on this list. The listing states 5K video, 24MP photos that it describes as interpolated to 48MP, a 100 ft waterproof case, two 1350mAh batteries, support for cards up to 128GB, an external microphone and HDMI output. It also lists 6-axis gyro EIS. It is rated 3.4 from 318 buyers, which is low, so look through the negative reviews for the problems people report most.

Who it suits: buyers on the tightest budget who care about the spec sheet and plenty of battery. Do not choose it for the 5K number alone.

### IZI ONE Plus 5K 48MP (₹8,549)

The listing gives 5K at 30 fps and 4K at 60 fps, 48MP photos, 6-axis gyro stabilisation, a 2-inch touch display plus a 1.3-inch front screen, a Type-C port and a Type-C microphone. It also advertises a 110 ft case, a 1-year warranty and more than 20 accessories. It has the lowest rating here, 3.3 from 405 buyers.

Who it suits: vloggers who want a front screen and a lot of accessories, and who are happy to accept more risk. Treat the 48MP figure with caution, as budget cameras often reach it by interpolation.

## Which one should you buy?

- **Best all-rounder:** AKASO EK7000 at ₹7,999. Highest rating, a remote and a long track record.
- **Cheapest sensible choice:** the second AKASO EK7000 at ₹6,398, once you have checked what is in the box.
- **Smallest and lightest:** SJCAM C110 Plus, if you accept the thin review count.
- **Dual screens for vlogging:** FitSpark Eagle or IZI ONE Plus. The FitSpark is better rated.
- **Biking:** any model with EIS or a gyro works, but read stabilisation reviews first. Budget stabilisation is weaker than on a GoPro or DJI.
- **Underwater:** use the included case, and check the seals and rubber before every dive.

## Mistakes to avoid with a budget action camera

- **Trusting resolution numbers.** Many cheap cameras label 5K or 48MP, then interpolate from a smaller sensor. Compare sample clips instead.
- **Skipping the microSD card.** Buy a branded, high-speed card, since some listings do not include one. Check the maximum card size too.
- **Forgetting the waterproof case.** Most of these cameras are splash-proof only without the case. Wipe the seals dry and keep them clean.
- **Expecting good night footage.** Small sensors struggle in the dark. Shoot in daylight when you can.
- **Buying one battery.** Runtime is limited, and a second battery matters more than a fancier mount.

## Should you spend more?

If you shoot often, care about low-light footage or need strong stabilisation, a camera above ₹10,000 from a major brand will be noticeably better. For occasional bike rides, trips and monsoon clips, one of the cameras above is enough.

## What to expect from any camera at this price

At this price, 4K means different things across brands, and megapixel and 5K claims are marketing. Ratings and sample videos are a better guide.

- **Stabilisation.** EIS or a 6-axis gyro smooths shake, but is weaker than premium models.
- **Waterproofing.** Often needs the included case.
- **Battery.** Carry a spare.
- **Storage.** Check the max microSD size.
