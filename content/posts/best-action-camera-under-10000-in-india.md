## What to expect

At this price, 4K means different things across brands, and megapixel and 5K claims are marketing. Ratings and sample videos are a better guide.

- **Stabilisation.** EIS or a 6-axis gyro smooths shake, but is weaker than premium models.
- **Waterproofing.** Often needs the included case.
- **Battery.** Carry a spare.
- **Storage.** Check the max microSD size.
