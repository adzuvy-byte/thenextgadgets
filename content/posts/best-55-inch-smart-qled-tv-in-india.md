## What the names mean

- **QLED:** an LCD TV with a quantum dot layer for richer colour. It is the standard budget-to-mid choice.
- **Mini LED:** a backlight made of far smaller LEDs in many dimming zones, so dark scenes look deeper than on a standard LED backlight.
- **QD-Mini LED:** Mini LED plus a quantum dot layer, as TCL calls it.
- **QNED:** LG's name for a Mini LED TV with a NanoCell colour filter.
- **Neo QLED:** Samsung's Mini LED plus quantum dot line.

## Which one should you buy?

- **Best features for the money, on paper:** the TCL 55Q6C (144Hz, 512+ zones, Onkyo sound, IMAX Enhanced) and the Xiaomi X Pro (Dolby Vision, 120Hz game booster).
- **Longest software support:** the Samsung Mini LED lists 7 years of OS upgrades.
- **Louder built-in sound:** the Hisense (48W with Dolby Atmos) and the TCL (46W Onkyo 2.1). Even so, a soundbar is a bigger upgrade. See our [soundbar guide](/best-soundbar-under-10000-in-india/).
- **Brand and service:** Samsung, LG, Sony and Philips have wide service networks. For lesser-known brands, check service coverage in your city.

## How to choose a 55-inch TV

- **Panel type.** Mini LED has better contrast than standard QLED, but costs more.
- **Refresh rate.** 60Hz is fine for TV and films. 120Hz or 144Hz helps with gaming, and the TCL and Xiaomi list it.
- **HDR.** Dolby Vision and HDR10+ matter on streaming apps. Check which your model supports.
- **Operating system.** Google TV has the widest app choice. webOS, Tizen and VIDAA each have their own app stores.
- **Viewing distance.** For 55 inches, about 6 to 8 feet suits 4K well.
- **Warranty.** Compare panel warranty terms, not just the product warranty.
