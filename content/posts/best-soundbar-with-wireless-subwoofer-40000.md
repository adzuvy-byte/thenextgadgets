## How to choose

- **Channels.** 2.1 is simple. 3.1 adds a centre channel. 5.1 adds rear speakers for surround.
- **Wireless sub.** Pairs without a cable and can sit anywhere near a socket.
- **Dolby and DTS.** Check what your TV and streaming box support.
- **HDMI ARC or eARC.** Makes volume control easy.
- **Brand.** Sony, Samsung and JBL have wider service.
