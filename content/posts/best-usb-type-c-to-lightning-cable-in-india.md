## What to check

- **MFi certification.** It means Apple-approved. Non-MFi cables can stop working after iOS updates.
- **Length.** 1 m is standard. Longer is handier, but check the charging speed.
- **Charger.** You need a USB-C charger that supports Power Delivery for fast charging.
- **Braiding.** Braided cables last longer.
- **Genuine.** Buy from official stores to avoid fakes.
