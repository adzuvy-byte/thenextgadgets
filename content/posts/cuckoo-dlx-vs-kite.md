Cuckoo DLX vs Kite is the choice you face if you like the idea of a Cuckoo air purifier and have narrowed it to its two mid-range models, the Respure DLX and the OmniPure Kite. They sit only ₹2,117 apart on Amazon.in, they share the same three-layer filter, the same 53 dB top noise level and the same 8-year motor warranty, and their airflow numbers are within 8% of each other. That makes it hard to see what the extra money buys. This comparison goes through the 13 Nov 2026 listings line by line to find out.

We have not tested either machine. The sources are the Amazon.in listings, Cuckoo's stated specs and our own arithmetic. Where a detail is missing, we say so.

## The short answer

- **Buy the Respure DLX** if you want the most airflow per rupee and a digital AQI display, for a room up to about 300 sq ft. It is also the one that, per its listing, ships with an additional free filter.
- **Buy the OmniPure Kite** if your room is 300 to 350 sq ft, or you want 360° air intake and an automatic PM2.5 and PM10 sensor, and the extra ₹2,117 does not bother you.
- **If the room is much bigger** than about 350 sq ft, neither is the answer. The Kite's bigger sibling, the OmniPure Ultra, is covered in our [Cuckoo air purifier guide](/cuckoo-air-purifier-india/).

## Airflow: the gap is smaller than the price gap

The Kite's CADR is 467 m³/h and the DLX's is 434 m³/h, a difference of 33 m³/h or 7.6%. The Kite costs 11.8% more. Put another way:

- DLX: ₹17,943 ÷ 434 = about **₹41 per m³/h** of airflow.
- Kite: ₹20,060 ÷ 467 = about **₹43 per m³/h**.

Both are good by that measure. For comparison, the Qubo Q600 works out to ₹33 per m³/h and the Mijia Purifier 6 to about ₹41.

The coverage claims scale the same way: 600 against 646 sq ft in 15 minutes, which is again about 8%. That is a useful reality check. The Kite is not a different class of machine, just a slightly stronger version.

To turn CADR into a room size, divide it by 5 to get the room volume that is cleaned five times an hour, then convert for a 10 ft ceiling:

- DLX: 434 ÷ 5 = 87 m³, about **305 sq ft**.
- Kite: 467 ÷ 5 = 93 m³, about **330 sq ft**.

Neither purifier will actually clean "600 sq ft in 15 minutes" at a meaningful rate. Those are best-case figures from a low number of air changes. In real winter smog, plan on about 250 to 300 sq ft for the DLX and 300 to 350 sq ft for the Kite. Our guide to [what CADR means](/what-is-cadr-in-air-purifier/) shows the calculation.

## What the extra ₹2,117 buys on the Kite

Going by the listing, three differences stand out:

1. **360° air intake.** The Kite pulls air from all directions, which should let you put it nearer the middle of the room or in a corner without as much loss. Cuckoo's listing for the DLX does not state an intake design.
2. **An automatic PM2.5 and PM10 sensor.** The Kite adjusts its fan by itself from dust readings. The DLX listing does not describe an auto sensor, though the base Respure's manual lists an Auto mode, so the DLX probably has one. Confirm with the seller if auto mode matters to you.
3. **A slightly higher CADR** (7.6%).

On the other side, the DLX has a **digital AQI display** that shows air quality on the unit, and its listing mentions **one additional free filter**. The asterisk next to that line means there are terms, so ask the seller what is included and whether the free filter is a genuine Cuckoo part. If you do get a second filter, it could be worth around ₹3,000 and more than offset the Kite's higher price.

## Noise and power

Both list about 20 dB in sleep mode and 53 dB at the top speed, which is clearly audible, about the level of an air-conditioner on high. The Kite adds a 35.7 dB normal mode that most people will use during the day. Both draw up to 60 W.

That 60 W deserves attention. At 12 hours a day and ₹8 a unit, it comes to about ₹173 a month at full draw. Most purifiers in this price band draw 27 to 40 W. A purifier on auto rarely runs flat out, so your real bill will be lower, but plan on a higher electricity cost than with a Coway or Philips.

## Filters and running cost

Both machines share the same layered approach: a washable pre-filter, a True HEPA H13 and an activated carbon or deodorising layer. We did not find a genuine replacement filter price for either model on Amazon.in on 13 Nov 2026. The base Cuckoo Respure's replacement filter is listed at ₹2,990, which is a reasonable guide but not proof for the DLX and Kite, whose filters may differ in size.

Before you buy, ask the seller for three things: the genuine filter price, where to buy it, and how long it takes to arrive. If a purifier's filter cannot be bought, the machine is only as good as its first filter.

## Ratings, price and trust: read the small print

All three Cuckoo purifiers on Amazon.in show the same 4.4 out of 5 from 710 ratings. That is a pooled score across the brand's linked listings, not a verdict on the DLX or the Kite, so it should not influence the choice between them.

The "list prices" of ₹40,990 and ₹45,990 are also worth ignoring. Selling prices of ₹17,943 and ₹20,060 mean the listings show a discount of 56% off, but those higher figures are not prices at which we have seen these machines sold. Judge each purifier by what it costs now.

Cuckoo is a long-established Korean home-appliance maker, better known in India for water purifiers. We could not find a published service network for its air purifiers, so the 8-year motor warranty is only as good as the support behind it. Ask the seller how a warranty claim works in your city before you pay.

## Who should buy which

**Buy the Respure DLX if:**
- The room is up to about 300 sq ft and you want the lowest price per unit of airflow.
- You like a number on the unit showing air quality.
- The additional free filter is confirmed.

**Buy the OmniPure Kite if:**
- The room is 300 to 350 sq ft, such as a large living room.
- You want 360° intake and a sensor that adjusts fan speed on its own.
- You will pay a little extra for the most airflow in the pair.

**Look elsewhere if:** you want the quietest bedroom machine or the lowest electricity cost.
