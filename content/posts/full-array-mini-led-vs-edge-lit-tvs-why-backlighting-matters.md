## What is Edge-Lit TV?

**Edge-Lit TVs** place their LED backlights along the edges of the screen, directing light inward through a diffuser. This design is thin and cost-effective but comes with compromises:

- **Uneven brightness** — corners may appear brighter, and center darker
- **Poor black uniformity** — light can bleed into dark scenes (known as "light blooming")
- **Lower peak brightness** — struggles in bright rooms or HDR content
- **Limited local dimming zones** — less control over contrast in small areas

**How it works:** LEDs are placed only along the edges of the TV panel, and light is spread across the screen using a diffuser.

**Key Issues:**

- **Uneven Brightness:** Dark scenes may look washed out, and bright spots (blooming) can appear near light sources.
- **Poor Local Dimming:** Limited control over specific dark and bright areas.
- **Lower HDR Impact:** Cannot match the punchy brightness of Mini LED.
- **Light Bleed:** Noticeable in dark rooms during high-contrast scenes.

**Typical Use:** Found in mid-range TVs where manufacturers cut costs to offer large screen sizes at lower prices.

## What is Full Array Mini LED?

**Full Array Mini LED TVs** place thousands of tiny LEDs directly behind the screen in multiple zones. Each zone can be dimmed or brightened independently, allowing for:

- **Superior contrast** – deep blacks next to bright highlights
- **Better brightness uniformity** – no distracting bright edges
- **Higher HDR performance** – great for movies, gaming, and sports
- **Improved viewing angles** – more consistent colors across the screen

### Full Array Mini LED – The Premium Standard

**How it works:** LEDs are spread evenly behind the entire panel, often using thousands of **Mini LEDs** with hundreds of local dimming zones.

**Advantages:**

- **Superior Contrast:** Deep blacks without crushing shadow details.
- **Better HDR Brightness:** More impactful highlights for HDR10, Dolby Vision, and HDR10+.
- **Uniform Lighting:** Consistent brightness from corner to corner.
- **Reduced Blooming:** Better light control around bright objects.

**Mini LED Upgrade:** Full Array backlight becomes even better when using **Mini LEDs**, as they allow far more dimming zones in the same space, delivering OLED-like black levels but with higher brightness.

## Full Array Mini LED vs Edge-Lit TV — Key Differences

| Feature | **Full Array Mini LED** | **Edge-Lit LED** |
|---|---|---|
| **Brightness Uniformity** | Excellent — no edge glow | Often uneven, especially in dark scenes |
| **Black Levels & Contrast** | Deep blacks, precise local dimming | Light bleeding in dark areas |
| **HDR Performance** | Bright and impactful | Limited punch in highlights |
| **Local Dimming Zones** | Hundreds to thousands | Few zones, less precision |
| **Screen Thickness** | Slightly thicker | Ultra-thin designs |
| **Price** | Usually higher | More affordable |

## Why Prefer Full Array Mini LED Over Edge-Lit

If you watch a lot of movies, HDR content, or play games, **Full Array Mini LED** is a far better choice because:

- It offers **cinema-like black levels** without distracting light bleed.
- It handles **bright and dark scenes in the same frame** beautifully.
- HDR truly pops, making the upgrade worth the price.
