## How to choose

- **Capacity.** 200 to 250L suits a family of three to four, 250L+ for five or more.
- **Star rating.** More stars mean lower electricity bills. A 3-star is a good balance.
- **Convertible mode.** Lets you turn the freezer into fridge space.
- **Inverter compressor.** Quieter and more efficient.
- **Warranty.** Check compressor warranty, and the product warranty.
- **Door width.** Measure your doorway and kitchen space.

## What to know before buying a double door fridge

- **Capacity is about people, not litres alone.** The listings suggest 223 to 256 litres for 2 to 3 members and 241 to 276 litres for 3 to 4. If you buy in bulk or cook daily, go a size up.
- **Star ratings change the bill.** A 3-star fridge uses roughly 195 to 202 units a year on these listings. A 2-star model uses more. Over ten years the difference in electricity adds up, so a lower price today is not always cheaper.
- **Convertible modes are practical.** They let you turn freezer space into fridge space during festivals and holidays. The mode that matters is how easily you can switch.
- **Inverter compressors are quieter.** They vary speed instead of switching on and off. Look at the compressor warranty, which is where most long-term cost lies.
- **Measure before you order.** Check your door width, the lift and the kitchen corner. Leave the clearance the manual recommends so that heat can escape.
- **Install and service.** Check installation is included and that the brand has a service centre in your city. A 5-year comprehensive warranty, as on the Godrej, helps if service is a worry.

## Should you spend more?

If you need more than 300 litres, a side-by-side or a premium brand brings better cooling and finishes at a higher price. For a family of two to four, the fridges above are the usual range.
