## Overview

- **70mai A810**: A premium 4K dashcam with advanced night vision, a supercapacitor, and a wider field of view. Ideal for drivers prioritizing top-tier video quality and reliability.
- **70mai A510**: A budget-friendly 1440p option with essential features like HDR and ADAS, perfect for everyday use without breaking the bank.

## Key Features Comparison

## 1. Video Quality

- **Resolution**: **A810**: 4K UHD (3840 x 2160) for crisp detail, crucial for license plate recognition. **A510**: 1440p (2560 x 1440), which is sharp but less detailed than 4K.
- **Sensor**: **A810**: Sony STARVIS 2 (IMX678) for superior low-light performance. **A510**: Sony STARVIS 2 (IMX675), decent but less advanced in dark conditions.
- **Field of View (FOV)**: **A810**: 150° FOV captures more scenery. **A510**: 140° FOV, slightly narrower but still adequate.
- **Night Vision**: Both have HDR, but the A810’s f/1.8 aperture and sensor excel in low light.

## 2. Storage & Recording

- **Max Storage**: Both support 128GB microSD cards.
- **Loop Recording**: Standard on both, overwriting old footage automatically.
- **Emergency Recording**: Built-in G-sensors trigger incident locks. The A810 adds **Time-Lapse Parking Mode** for extended monitoring.

## 3. Advanced Features

- **ADAS**: Both include lane departure/collision alerts, but the A810’s algorithms are more refined.
- **GPS**: Built-in for speed/route tracking.
- **Voice Control**: Both support basic commands (e.g., “Take photo”), but the A810 responds faster.
- **Connectivity**: Wi-Fi/app pairing for both, though the A810 transfers 4K files quicker.

## 4. Design & Build

- **Display**: **A810**: 3-inch screen for clearer previews. **A510**: Compact 2-inch display.
- **Build**: **A810**: Supercapacitor withstands extreme temperatures. **A510**: Lithium battery may degrade faster in heat.
- **Size**: The A810 is bulkier, while the A510 is more discreet.

## Pros & Cons

## 70mai A810

- **Pros**: 4K resolution, stellar night vision, supercapacitor, wide FOV.
- **Cons**: Pricier, larger size.

## 70mai A510

- **Pros**: Affordable, compact, reliable 1440p.
- **Cons**: Lower resolution, battery less durable.

## Which Should You Buy?

- **Choose the A810 if**: You want future-proof 4K recording, drive frequently at night, or need robust parking monitoring.
- **Choose the A510 if**: You seek a budget-friendly option with solid daytime performance and essential features.

## Which One Should You Choose?

## Go for the 70mai A810 if:

✔️ You want **true 4K UHD recording** for the Best video quality. ✔️ You drive frequently at **night or in low-light conditions**. ✔️ You need a **wider field of view (150°)**. ✔️ You prefer **top-tier performance with enhanced HDR**.

The **A810** is a powerhouse for enthusiasts and professionals, while the **A510** offers great value for casual users.
