## How we picked them

We searched Amazon.in for "best air purifier under 15000" and read the first three pages of results. We removed sponsored listings, filters and accessories, car and portable purifiers, listings with no usable specs, and anything priced at ₹15,000 or more. Where a search price and the product page disagreed, we dropped the product. We then ranked what was left by airflow (CADR), buyer ratings, filter cost and noise. We have not tested these purifiers ourselves. Our opinions are based on published specs, buyer ratings and how each model compares with the others.

## The short answer

- **Best overall:** Coway Airmega 150 (₹14,999). The highest rating we found, with cheap, long-life filters.

- **Best value with a big review base:** Xiaomi 4 Lite (₹10,990) or Honeywell Air Touch V5 (₹11,499).

## Which one should you buy?

**For a bedroom (up to 200 sq ft):** the Philips AC0950 or the Coway Airmega 100.
**For a living room (200 to 350 sq ft):** the Coway Airmega 150.
**For a large hall (over 350 sq ft) or heavy Delhi NCR smog:** the Qubo Q600, the Cuckoo Respure or the Honeywell V5 for airflow, accepting the trade-offs in noise and service.
**For the lowest yearly running cost:** the Coway Airmega 150, Cuckoo Respure or Honeywell V5. They have the cheapest filters.

## Three-year cost, not just the price

A purifier's price is only part of what you pay. With two filter changes over three years, using genuine filter prices where we could confirm them:

- Coway Airmega 150: ₹14,999 + 2 x ₹3,299 = ₹21,597.
- Philips AC0950: ₹11,989 + 2 x ₹2,720 = ₹17,429.
- Philips AC1711: ₹14,399 + 2 x ₹4,995 = ₹24,389.
- Cuckoo Respure: ₹13,990 + 2 x ₹2,990 = ₹19,970 (if replaced yearly).
- Honeywell V5: ₹11,499 + 2 x ₹2,500 = ₹16,499 (filter price per MySmartPrice).

## What to look for in this price band

- **CADR over coverage claims.** Our guide to [what CADR means](/what-is-cadr-in-air-purifier/) explains why.
- **A real carbon layer** if cooking smells or smoke are your concern, because HEPA does not remove gases.
- **No ionizer or ozone-producing function.** The EPA advises avoiding purifiers that emit ozone.
- **A filter you can actually buy.** Check the price and availability of the replacement.
- **Service.** Outside the metros, a brand with a local service network is worth paying a little more for.
