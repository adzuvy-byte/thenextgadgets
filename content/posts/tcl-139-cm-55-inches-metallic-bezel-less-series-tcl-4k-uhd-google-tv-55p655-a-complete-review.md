TCL has been making waves in the smart TV industry with its affordable yet feature-packed televisions. The **TCL 4K UHD Google TV 55P655** is no exception, bringing a **4K Ultra HD resolution, Google TV integration, and a bezel-less design** at a competitive price. In this review, we will dive deep into the features, specifications, pros, and cons of this television.

## Design & Build Quality

One of the standout features of the **TCL 4K UHD Google TV 55P655** is its **metallic bezel-less design**, which gives it a sleek and modern look. The ultra-slim bezels enhance the viewing experience by making the display appear more immersive. The sturdy **metallic build** ensures durability, while the minimalistic stand design complements modern interiors.

## Display & Picture Quality

The TCL 4K UHD Google TV 55P655 features a **55-inch 4K Ultra HD LED panel** with a resolution of **3840 x 2160 pixels**. The TV supports **HDR10**, ensuring vibrant colors and enhanced contrast. With **Dynamic Color Enhancement and Micro Dimming**, the picture quality remains consistent across various lighting conditions.

### Key Display Features:

- **4K Ultra HD resolution (3840 x 2160 pixels)**
- **HDR10 support** for enhanced colors and contrast
- **60Hz refresh rate** for smooth visuals
- **Dynamic Color Enhancement** for lifelike colors
- **Micro Dimming** to optimize brightness and contrast

## Audio Performance

Equipped with **Dolby Audio**, the TCL 4K UHD Google TV 55P655 offers **20W stereo speakers** that provide clear and immersive sound. While the built-in speakers are decent for casual viewing, an external sound system or soundbar may be required for a more cinematic experience.

### Key Audio Features:

- **Dolby Audio support** for rich sound output
- **20W stereo speakers** for clear and balanced sound
- **AI Sound Optimization** adjusts audio based on content

This TV runs on **Google TV**, giving users access to thousands of apps, streaming services, and Google Play Store content. The **built-in Google Assistant** enables voice commands, making navigation easy. Additionally, Chromecast is built-in, allowing seamless casting from smartphones and tablets.

### Key Smart Features:

- **TCL 4K UHD Google TV** interface with access to apps like Netflix, YouTube, Prime Video, and more
