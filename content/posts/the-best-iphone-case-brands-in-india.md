## How to choose a case

- **Protection.** TPU with air cushions or a hard back helps with drops. Thin silicone only protects against scratches.
- **MagSafe.** A magnetic case works with MagSafe chargers and accessories.
- **Clear or coloured.** Clear shows the phone but can yellow.
- **Camera lip.** A raised edge protects the lens.
- **Grip and weight.** Silicone is grippier. Hard plastic is lighter.
