## How to choose

- **Cheap mini (₹500):** fine for light dust, small crumbs and seats.
- **Cordless 6000mAh (₹3,000+):** stronger suction and a longer run time.
- **Extras:** blower mode and a 12V option are useful.

## What to check

- **Run time.** Check the battery capacity, in mAh.
- **Suction claims.** Pa and watt numbers are not directly comparable.
- **Attachments.** A crevice tool helps the most.
- **Filter.** A washable filter saves money.
