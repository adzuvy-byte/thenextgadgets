## How to choose a dashcam

- **Front only or front and rear.** Dual channel records both, which helps in rear-end claims.
- **Resolution.** 2K is a sensible step up from 1080p. "4K upscaled" is not native 4K, so read the title closely.
- **Night video.** Look for a STARVIS or STARVIS 2 sensor and a wide aperture.
- **Wi-Fi and app.** Makes it easy to copy clips to your phone.
- **Memory card.** Most need a separate microSD card, so check what is in the box.

## What to check

- **Parking mode.** Needs a hardwire kit on most models.
- **Warranty and service.** Prefer brands with Indian support.
- **Installation.** The cable should route cleanly along the windscreen.
