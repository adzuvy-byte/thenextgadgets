## How to choose

- **Wireless subwoofer:** the 9330 states it in the title. Confirm on the listing for the others.
- **Surround:** the 5.1 models add rear speakers but need space and wires.
- **Single bar:** the JBL is the simplest to set up.

## What to check

- **Inputs.** HDMI ARC, optical and Bluetooth.
- **Wattage claims.** Peak watts are not RMS watts.
- **Room size.** Big watt numbers matter in larger rooms.
