## Ink tank or cartridge?

- **Ink tank (MegaTank, Smart Tank, Brother T-series):** costs more upfront, far cheaper per page. Best if you print regularly.
- **Cartridge inkjet:** cheap to buy, expensive per page. Fine for rare printing.

## What to check

- **WiFi.** Needed for phone printing and no cable.
- **Running cost.** Look for pages per ink set.
- **Scan and copy.** All of these include it.
- **Duplex and ADF.** Rarely in this price range.
- **Dry nozzles.** Print at least weekly or the nozzles clog.
