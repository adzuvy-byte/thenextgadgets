## How to choose a soundbar at this price

- **Check the TV connection.** HDMI ARC or eARC is the easiest, and optical or AUX work on most TVs. Check the ports on your TV before you buy.
- **Read wattage carefully.** Watt figures on listings are not comparable across brands. RMS is the honest figure. Peak (PMPO-style) numbers tend to look bigger than the real output.
- **Subwoofer.** A separate or wireless subwoofer gives far better bass than a built-in one.
- **Rear speakers.** They add surround, but you need space and cables behind the sofa.
- **Dialogue.** A centre channel helps with voices, which matters for films and OTT shows.
- **Room size.** Small rooms do not need 500W of anything.
