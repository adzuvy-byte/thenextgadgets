Qubo vs Coway vs Philips is the question behind a lot of air purifier shopping in India, because the three brands take three different approaches. Qubo (from the Hero Group) sells high-CADR smart purifiers at mid prices. Coway, a Korean maker, sells fewer models but has the strongest buyer ratings in this price band. Philips sells quiet, compact purifiers with a 2-year warranty and a premium price. This guide compares them on what matters when you pay: air delivered per rupee, filter running cost, noise, warranty and how well each suits a given room.

Every price, rating and spec below was checked on Amazon.in or the manufacturer's page on 11 Nov 2026. We name the model for each claim, because brand-level generalisations are where most buying guides go wrong.

## Qubo vs Coway vs Philips: the short answer

- **Best for most homes: Coway Airmega 150.** ₹14,999, rated 4.7 out of 5 by 6,821 buyers, with a ₹3,299 genuine filter.
- **Best for a big, smoggy room: Qubo Q600.** 450 m³/h CADR at ₹14,990.
- **Best for a quiet bedroom: Philips AC0950 or AC1711.** Philips publishes sleep-mode noise as low as 15 to 20 dB and gives a 2-year warranty.

## Brand profiles

### Qubo (Hero Group)

Qubo is Hero Group's smart-home brand and sells purifiers numbered by their claimed coverage: Q200 (200 sq ft), Q400, Q500, Q600 and Q1000 (1,000 sq ft). On Amazon.in the Q200 was ₹7,490, Q500 ₹11,990, Q600 ₹14,990 and Q1000 ₹19,990 on 11 Nov 2026. The range is app-controlled and works with Alexa and Google Assistant. Qubo gives a 1-year warranty and sells filters through its own store, and recommends changing the filter about once a year. The trade-off is that Qubo does not publish noise or filter prices clearly on its pages, so ask before you buy.

### Coway

Coway (see our full [Coway air purifier guide](/coway-air-purifier-india/)) is a Korean brand with a deliberately small India lineup. The Airmega 150 (355 sq ft) uses a Green True HEPA filter with a carbon layer and is rated 8,500 hours, and the genuine replacement is ₹3,299. The cheaper Airmega 100 (₹11,999, CADR 240, 270 sq ft) suits a bedroom. Coway's strength is buyer satisfaction rather than headline CADR.

### Philips

Philips sells under the Versuni brand in India. Its models run from the AC0950 (₹11,989) through the AC1711 and AC1715 (300 m³/h CADR) to the AC3220 (520 m³/h). Philips publishes noise, power and CADR figures clearly, offers a 2-year warranty and uses NanoProtect filters. The weaknesses are price per CADR and a genuine filter that costs ₹4,995 for the AC1711 and AC1715 range.

## Filter cost over three years

A purifier is a subscription to filters. Taking each maker's recommended yearly change, here is the three-year cost with two filter swaps, using the prices above.

- Coway Airmega 150: ₹14,999 + 2 x ₹3,299 = ₹21,597.
- Philips AC1711: ₹14,399 + 2 x ₹4,995 = ₹24,389.
- Philips AC0950: ₹11,989 + 2 x ₹2,720 = ₹17,429.
- Qubo Q600: ₹14,990 + two filters at a price we could not verify.

Heavy smog shortens filter life, so in Delhi NCR budget for an annual change rather than the 8,500 or 9,000 hours on the box. The cheapest filter here belongs to the smallest Philips, the AC0950, which is a bedroom model. See [Philips filter costs in detail](/philips-ac1711-filter-philips-ac1715-filter-complete-guide/).

## Noise and sleep

Philips publishes the clearest figures: the AC1711 and AC1715 run from 15 dB in sleep mode to 50 dB, and the AC0950 is rated about 20 dB in sleep mode. Qubo does not publish a decibel range on the pages we checked. Because noise rises with airflow, a 450 m³/h machine such as the Q600 will be louder at top speed than a 250 to 300 m³/h one. For a bedroom, choose by sleep-mode noise, not by headline CADR.

## Apps and smart features

All three brands offer app control on selected models. Philips uses the Air+ app on the AC1715 (the AC1711 has no Wi-Fi). Qubo's app covers all its smart models and adds Alexa and Google Assistant. Coway's Airmega 150 is a straightforward machine with an air quality indicator, and its value comes from filtering, not smart features. If you want remote control and schedules, Qubo or the Philips AC1715 will serve you; if you want a purifier you never touch, the Coway is the simple choice.

## Warranty and service

Philips gives 2 years on its purifiers, with a paid 1-year extension. Qubo gives 1 year, with full terms on its warranty page. Check the live Amazon listing for Coway's warranty terms, since they differ by seller and variant. Service reach matters more than the warranty length if you live outside a metro, so check that a service centre exists in your city before you buy.

- **Bedroom or small living room up to 350 sq ft:** Coway Airmega 150, or Philips AC1711 for quiet.
- **Living room 350 to 500 sq ft:** Qubo Q600, or the Philips AC3220 if the budget is higher.
