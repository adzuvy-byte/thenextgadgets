### 1. Brand Overview & Philosophy

- **Qubo**: An Indian / Hero-Group brand focused on **smart, budget-friendly** purifiers. They emphasize app control, voice integration (Alexa/Google), and long filter life in well-priced units.
- **Coway**: A Korean brand known for premium build quality, highly efficient HEPA systems, and proven performance. Their purifiers often have long filter life and quiet operation.
- **Philips**: A global electronics leader. Their air purifiers focus on scientific filtration, strong CADR, and reliable sensors, with well-tested performance and long filter warranties.

## 4. Real-World Use: Pros & Cons Compared

**Qubo**

- **Pros**: Affordable, good for smart homes, configurable for different room sizes, low running cost.
- **Cons**: Some users on Reddit have reported **sensor accuracy issues** and poor customer support.

**Coway**

- **Pros**: Very reliable, effective HEPA filtration, long filter life, quiet operation, good support for serious allergy or pollution cleanup.
- **Cons**: Premium pricing; some users report differences in stated room coverage vs real-world performance.

**Philips**

- **Pros**: Excellent build and filtration, consistent performance, wide model range, long filter intervals.
- **Cons**: Filter replacement can be costly; high CADR models are more expensive.

## 5. Which Brand / Model Should You Choose — Based on Your Needs

Here’s what we suggest depending on different priorities:

- **If you want a budget purifier with smart features** → go for **Qubo Q400 or Q500**.
- **If you need a purifier for a large room and want long-term reliability** → pick a **Coway AirMega model** (or the Mighty for smaller rooms).
- **If you want top-tier purification, especially for allergens, VOCs or ultra-fine particles** → choose a **Philips model like the 3000i or higher**.

## 6. Final Thoughts

- **Qubo** is the value-for-money, smart, made-for-India option.
- **Coway** is for serious air quality users who want trusted HEPA performance.
- **Philips** offers premium purification with large CADR and long filter life backed by a global brand.

Each brand has something unique, so pick based on your room size, budget, and how seriously you take your indoor air.
