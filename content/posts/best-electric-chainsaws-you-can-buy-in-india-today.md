## How to choose

- **Corded or cordless.** Corded saws give steady power but need a socket. Cordless are portable but limited by the battery.
- **Bar length.** 6 to 8 inches for pruning, 12 to 16 inches for thicker branches.
- **Auto oiling.** Keeps the chain lubricated.
- **Safety.** Wear gloves, eye and ear protection. Never use one above shoulder height.
- **Use.** These are for light home use, not for felling large trees.
