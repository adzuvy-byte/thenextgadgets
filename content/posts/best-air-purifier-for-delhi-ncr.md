Delhi NCR winters are hard on indoor air. A [Centre for Science and Environment (CSE) analysis](https://www.downtoearth.org.in/air/delhi-had-only-one-good-air-day-in-92-days-despite-5769-mm-of-rainfall-cse-analysis), released in September 2026, found just one "Good" air day in Delhi across the June to August monsoon, and none in April and May. Winter is worse. The US EPA says indoor levels of some pollutants [may be two to five times, and occasionally more than 100 times, higher than outdoor levels](https://www.epa.gov/iaq-schools/why-indoor-air-quality-important-schools). That is an EPA finding on buildings in general and not a measurement of your home.

An air purifier will not fix the air outside, but it can cut the fine particles you breathe indoors. This guide is for people in Delhi, Noida, Gurugram, Ghaziabad, Faridabad, Lucknow, Patna and other polluted cities. We picked purifiers on the things that matter in heavy smog: airflow (CADR), the carbon layer, how long the filter lasts, and what the filter costs. Prices, ratings and specs were checked on Amazon.in on 11 Nov 2026 and will change, so check the live listing before you buy.

## How to size a purifier for smog months

Do not trust the coverage number on the box. Makers often calculate it for slow air changes, which is too gentle for heavy pollution. Use CADR instead.

1. Work out your room's volume: floor area in square metres times ceiling height. A 150 sq ft bedroom is about 14 m² and with a 10 ft (3 m) ceiling is about 42 m³.
2. Multiply by 5 for heavy winter pollution (3 for everyday use). That gives about 210 m³/h for the bedroom.
3. Choose a purifier whose CADR is at least that number.

This is a rule of thumb. A bigger room, open doors or a leaky window need a higher CADR. Our [main guide to air purifiers by room size](/best-air-purifier-for-home-in-india/) has more detail and picks for different rooms.

## What matters most in heavy smog

- **CADR.** More airflow clears the room faster. This matters more than any coverage claim.
- **A real carbon layer.** HEPA traps particles. It does not remove gases, cooking fumes or incense smoke. Only activated carbon does, and a thin carbon sheet fills up fast.
- **Filter life and filter price.** In heavy smog, filters clog sooner than their rated life. A low-cost filter or a long filter life keeps the running cost down.
- **A live PM2.5 reading.** A number on the unit tells you if it is working. A colour light only gives a rough idea.
- **Noise at night.** Check the sleep-mode noise if it goes in a bedroom.
- **Ignore extras.** Ionisers and UV lights add little. The HEPA filter does the work.

## Using your purifier during Diwali smoke and stubble burning

- Keep the doors and windows of the room closed and run the purifier on auto or a high setting.
- Put it where air can flow around it, away from walls and curtains.
- Wipe or vacuum the pre-filter as the maker advises, since it clogs fastest in smog.
- A purifier will not clear a very leaky room. Seal gaps if you can.
- Replace filters when the indicator says so, and sooner if the unit is struggling.
