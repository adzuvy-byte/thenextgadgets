## How to choose

- **Chip.** Core i3-1305U and 1315U are 13th Gen. Core 3 100U is 14th Gen. All are fine for office, study and browsing.
- **RAM.** Choose 16GB if you can. 8GB is the minimum.
- **Storage.** 512GB SSD is better than 256GB.
- **Reviews.** Most 2026 models have few reviews, so use brand and warranty to guide you.

## What to check

- **Office and Windows.** Not every listing includes Office.
- **Display.** FHD at 250 nits or brighter.
- **Weight and battery.** Find both on the listing.
- **Service.** Check your city's service centre.
