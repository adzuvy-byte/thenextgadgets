## How to choose a 4K TV

- **Screen size and distance.** For a 43-inch TV, a viewing distance of about 1.2 to 2 metres works well. Move up to 50 inches or more if you sit further away.
- **HDR support is not HDR quality.** Budget LED TVs list HDR10, HDR10+ and Dolby Vision, but their panels are not bright enough to show HDR at its best. Treat these as compatibility labels, not guarantees.
- **Smart platform.** Google TV has the widest app support. Fire TV, Samsung, LG and Hisense's VIDAA use their own platforms, which are smooth but have a different app store. Check that your favourite OTT apps are available.
- **Sound.** Built-in speakers on budget TVs are basic. A soundbar helps for movies.
- **Ports and refresh rate.** Check the number of HDMI ports. Most TVs at this price are 60 Hz. The Xiaomi 55-inch lists a 120Hz Game Booster, so gamers should check HDMI and refresh rate details.
- **Warranty and service.** Check the warranty terms and whether the brand has service in your city.
