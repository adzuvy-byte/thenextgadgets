## How to choose a student laptop

- **RAM:** 8 GB is the minimum for comfortable use with many browser tabs. 16 GB gives more headroom for multitasking, coding and design tools.
- **Storage:** Choose an SSD. 512 GB is a comfortable size.
- **Processor:** For study use, a recent Core i3, Core 5, Ryzen 5 or similar is enough. Pay more only for heavy software such as video editing or 3D tools.
- **Weight and battery:** If you carry the laptop daily, a 14-inch model near 1.5 kg is easier than a 15.6-inch one.
- **Display:** Full HD is the baseline. Some budget listings still use 1366 x 768, so check the resolution.
- **Graphics card:** Needed for gaming, 3D and some engineering courses, not for general study.
- **Course needs:** Check whether your course needs a specific operating system or software.
- **Warranty and service:** Check the warranty and the brand's service centres in your city.
