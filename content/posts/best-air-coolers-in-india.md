## Personal, tower or desert?

- **Personal** coolers (up to about 40L) suit a bedroom or study.
- **Tower** coolers are slim and fit small floor spaces.
- **Desert** coolers (70L and up) cool big rooms and halls.

See our dedicated lists for [desert coolers](/best-desert-cooler-in-india/) and [personal coolers](/best-personal-aircooler-to-buy-in-india/).

## What to check

- **Climate.** Coolers work best in dry heat, not in humid weather.
- **Room size and tank size.** Pick the type to match the room.
- **Noise and power.** Check reviews on noise and watts.
