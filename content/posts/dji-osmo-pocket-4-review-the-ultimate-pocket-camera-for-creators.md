The DJI Osmo Pocket 4 is a pocket-sized gimbal camera built for vloggers and travel creators. It keeps the compact shape that made the Pocket 3 popular and adds better dynamic range and low-light performance, plus a larger battery. This page uses specs and verdicts from Digital Camera World's review and Tech Stories India's launch coverage. Indian prices change, so check live pricing on Amazon.in before you buy.

## Key specs

- **Sensor:** 1-inch CMOS.
- **Video:** up to 4K at 240 fps for slow motion, with 10-bit D-Log.
- **Stills:** up to 37 MP in JPEG or DNG.
- **Lens:** 20 mm equivalent wide angle.
- **Battery:** 1,545 mAh, rated at about 240 minutes.
- **Screen:** 2-inch rotating touchscreen, 1,000 nits.
- **Storage:** 107 GB built in, plus a microSD slot.
- **Weight:** 190.5 g, a little heavier than the Pocket 3 at 179 g.

## What is good

- **Battery life.** The review calls the battery life surprisingly good for something this small, and long clips are possible.
- **10-bit D-Log video** gives you more room to grade colour.
- **Built-in storage.** 107 GB means you can shoot without a memory card at first.
- **Image quality.** Better dynamic range and low-light performance than the Pocket 3.
- **Built-in beauty and glamour effects** for face-to-camera content.

## What to watch

- **Zoom is digital** at every level on the standard Pocket 4.
- **Menus can still be confusing,** according to the reviewer.
- **The protruding camera module is vulnerable,** so use the cover or a case.

Digital Camera World rated it 5/5 for features and design and 4/5 for performance and value.

## Pocket 4 vs Pocket 4P

DJI also sells the Osmo Pocket 4P, a dual-camera version. Tech Stories India reports:

| | Osmo Pocket 4P |
|---|---|
| Wide camera | 1-inch CMOS, 20 mm f/2.0 |
| Medium-tele camera | 1/1.28-inch, 60 mm f/1.8, 3x optical zoom |
| Dynamic range | Up to 17 stops (wide camera) |
| Video | 4K at 240 fps (wide), 4K at 200 fps (tele), 10-bit D-Log 2 |
| Tracking | ActiveTrack 8.0, up to 12x zoom |
| Storage | 103 GB built in |
| India price (Standard Combo) | ₹84,990 on Amazon India at the time of that report, MRP ₹99,990 |

The 4P is the one to look at if you want real optical zoom. It costs considerably more.

## Price in India

The 4P's Indian price above comes from an August 2026 report. For the standard Pocket 4, Indian reviews have discussed a price in the region of ₹55,000, but exact pricing varies by seller and combo, so please check the listing. Creator Combos include extra accessories and cost more. In the UK the standard model started at £445 and the Creator Combo at £549.

## Pocket 4 or Pocket 3?

If you already own a Pocket 3, the review says the upgrade may feel less compelling. If you are buying new, the Pocket 4 is a strong first choice in its category. See our [Pocket 4 vs Pocket 3 comparison](/dji-osmo-pocket-4-vs-pocket-3-which-one-should-you-buy/) for details.

## Who should buy it

- Vloggers and travellers who want stabilised video from a pocket camera.
- Creators who want better results than a phone in low light.
- Anyone who wants a simple, small camera that is quick to start.

Skip it if you need a long optical zoom (look at the 4P) or interchangeable lenses (look at a mirrorless camera). For other options, see our [best cameras for vlogging in India](/best-cameras-for-vlogging-in-india/).
