## Dashcam Buying Guide

Here's a dashcam buyer's guide to help you make an informed decision:

1. **Video Quality:** To ensure clear and detailed film, look for a dashcam with a high video resolution, preferably Full HD (1080p) or higher.
2. **Field of View:** A wider field of view, often 120 degrees or more, ensures that the road is covered more thoroughly and that more details are captured.
3. **Night Vision:** Choose a dashcam that has good night vision capabilities. To record clear footage in low-light or dark circumstances, look for features such as low-light sensors and infrared lights.
4. **GPS Functionality:** Some dashcams come with built-in GPS to record the vehicle's location and speed data. This information can be useful for insurance claims or providing evidence in case of accidents.
5. **Loop Recording:** When the memory card is full, this feature allows the dashcam to automatically overwrite earlier recordings. It ensures continuous recording without the need for manual file deletion.
6. **G-Sensor:** A G-sensor detects abrupt movements or impacts and records the film as an event file to prevent it from being overwritten. This function is useful for recording accidents or incidents.
7. **Parking Mode:**Dashcams with parking mode continue to record even when the car is parked. They are typically activated by motion or impact, enabling surveillance and proof in the event of a parking accident or vandalism.
8. **Wi-Fi Connectivity and Mobile App:** Look for dashcams that have Wi-Fi and a specialised mobile app. Using your smartphone or tablet, you can simply browse and download footage, alter settings, and share recordings.
9. **Mounting and Installation:** Consider the ease of installation and mounting options. Look for dashcams that come with adhesive mounts or suction cups for secure attachment to the windshield.
10. **Price and Budget:** Set a budget based on your needs and look for dashcams that offer the functionality you desire within that price range. Remember to think about the brand's total worth and dependability.
11. **Brand Reputation and Customer Reviews**: Research the brand's reputation for producing reliable dashcams. Read customer reviews to get insights into the performance, durability, and customer satisfaction of specific models.
12. **Legal Considerations:** To ensure compliance with any privacy or recording laws, familiarise yourself with dashcam rules in your country or state.

By considering these factors, you can select a dashcam that Best suits your needs and provides reliable video evidence in case of incidents or accidents on the road.
