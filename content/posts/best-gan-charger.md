## What is a GaN charger?

GaN stands for gallium nitride, a semiconductor that wastes less energy as heat than silicon. A cooler charger can be smaller and still output the same power, so a 65 W GaN charger is about the size of an older 20 W brick.

## How to choose

- **Total wattage.** When several ports are in use, the total power is shared. A 65 W charger might give 45 W to one port and 20 W to another.
- **Laptop needs.** Check your laptop's charger wattage. Many thin laptops use 45 to 65 W.
- **Ports.** Two USB-C ports suit a phone plus a laptop. Three ports help when travelling.
- **Protocols.** PD 3.0 covers most devices. PPS helps recent Samsung phones.
- **Cable.** A 60 W cable limits a higher power charger. See our [USB-C cable guide](/best-usb-c-cable-in-india/).
- **Safety.** Choose BIS certified chargers from known brands.
