## Why buy a Coway air purifier? The brand in brief

Coway is a South Korean maker of air and water purifiers. It sells a small range in India, and the same few models have been on sale for years. That is both its strength and its limit. On the strength side:

- **Ratings that stand out.** The Airmega 150 is rated 4.7 out of 5 by 6,821 buyers on Amazon.in. Most competing models in this price band sit between 3.8 and 4.4, usually with far fewer reviews.
- **Cheap, long-life filters.** Coway rates its Green True HEPA filter at 8,500 hours. The genuine replacement for the Airmega 150 is ₹3,299, against ₹4,995 for a Philips AC1715.
- **Filters you can handle without mess.** The Airmega 150 uses cartridge-style filters that come out in one piece.
- **Service support.** The Airmega 250 listing states free home installation and service from Coway India. Check that it covers your city before you buy.
- **Mechanical filtration only.** The Airmega 100 listing says it uses no ionisers or UV, so there is no ozone-producing component.

And the limits:

- **No smart features on the models we checked.** The listings mention air quality indicators and auto modes, but no Wi-Fi app. If app control matters, look at a [Qubo Q600](/qubo-q600-vs-philips-ac1715-smart-air-purifier-which-one-should-you-buy/) or a [Philips AC1715](/philips-ac1711-filter-philips-ac1715-filter-complete-guide/).
- **Less CADR per rupee at the top end.** The Qubo Q600 lists 450 m³/h for ₹14,990. Coway's strength is quality and running cost, not the biggest number.
- **A narrow range.** Four models cover bedrooms to large living rooms, so there is less choice than with Philips or Honeywell.
- **Claims are Coway's own.** Statements such as "the world's largest R&D centre in air care" and "kills 99.999% of viruses" come from the Amazon.in listings and have not been independently verified by us.

## The four Coway models, one by one

### Coway Airmega 150 (AP-1019C): the one most people should buy

This is Coway's best-selling air purifier in India, and the data backs it up. It uses a three-stage system: a pre-filter, a urethane carbon filter for odours, cooking smells and gases such as VOCs, and an anti-virus Green True HEPA filter rated for 8,500 hours. A four-colour LED shows air quality, an auto mode adjusts the fan, and noise runs from 22 to 49 dB, which is quiet enough for a bedroom. Coway says it suits rooms of up to 355 sq ft.

The genuine replacement filter (Coway Original Anti-Virus Green H13 True HEPA) costs ₹3,299 on Amazon.in, rated 4.4 by 93 buyers, and the listing says 8,500 hours is about 1.5 to 2 years of normal use. In heavy smog, plan to change it yearly.

**Where it falls short:** no app, and the listing does not publish a CADR. It will struggle in a very large hall.

### Coway Airmega 100 (AP-0823E): a quieter, smaller option

A step down in price (₹11,999) with a published CADR of 240 m³/h and coverage of 270 sq ft. It has a three-in-one True HEPA and carbon filter, ECARF-certified allergy care, a 20 dB sleep mode and a four-colour air quality light. Filters last about a year. Pick it for a bedroom up to about 200 sq ft where the Airmega 150 would be overkill. The ₹3,000 you save is small though, and the 150 has a far stronger rating, so most people should stretch to the 150.

### Coway Airmega 50 (AP-0425C): the cheapest entry

New in 2026 at ₹6,999, with a CADR of 120.1 m³/h, a HyperCaptive filter and a deodorisation filter, auto mode and 18.4 dB minimum noise. At 1.6 kg and 17 x 17 x 32 cm it fits on a desk or bedside table. It is the right choice only for a small bedroom, study or nursery. In a larger room the airflow will be too low.

### Coway Airmega 250 (AP-1720H): for big rooms

The largest model on Amazon.in at ₹31,999, rated 4.5 by 250 buyers. Coway claims 930 sq ft in 30 minutes, with Green True HEPA and activated carbon filters rated at 8,500 hours, an air quality indicator, Smart and Rapid modes and a timer. Price is the issue: for about ₹9,500 less you could buy the Philips AC3220 (520 m³/h, ₹22,480) and for ₹17,000 less a Qubo Q600 (450 m³/h). The Airmega 250 makes sense if you specifically want Coway's filters, service and low noise in a large room.

## How to get the best from a Coway purifier

1. Place it 15 to 30 cm from walls and keep the vent clear of curtains.
2. Use Auto mode in the day and the lowest speed at night.
3. Wipe the pre-filter every two to four weeks and the sensor monthly.
4. Keep doors and windows closed while it runs.
5. Buy genuine filters from Coway's Amazon.in brand listing, and replace them when the indicator lights up.
