## Qubo Dashcam Pro 4K – Overview

The **Qubo Car Dashcam Pro 4K** is built for drivers who want the highest level of clarity and safety. It features an **8MP front camera capable of True 4K recording** along with a **2MP rear camera (Full HD)** for complete front and rear monitoring.

Powered by the **Sony STARVIS IMX415 sensor**, it delivers exceptional video quality even in challenging lighting conditions.

## Key Features of Qubo 4K Dashcam Pro for Car

### 1. True 4K Ultra HD Front Camera

The standout feature of the **Qubo Car Dashcam Pro 4K** is its **True 4K resolution (3840×2160)** front camera. This ensures:

- Crystal-clear footage
- Easy identification of number plates
- Enhanced detail in recordings

The rear camera records in Full HD, offering balanced dual-channel coverage.

### 2. Sony STARVIS IMX415 Sensor (Best STARVIS Dashcam Feature)

The dashcam uses the **Sony STARVIS IMX415 sensor**, known for:

- Excellent low-light sensitivity
- Reduced noise in night footage
- Better dynamic range

This makes the **qubo 4k dashcam pro for car** a solid option among the **Best STARVIS dashcam** devices for night driving.

### 3. ADAS (Advanced Driver Assistance System)

One of the major upgrades in this dashcam is ADAS, which includes:

- Lane departure warning
- Forward collision alerts
- Driver safety notifications

These features help improve awareness and reduce the chances of accidents.

### 4. Dual Channel Recording

With simultaneous front and rear recording:

- Capture incidents from both angles
- Improve evidence in case of accidents
- Ensure full vehicle coverage

### 5. Built-in GPS & Wi-Fi Connectivity

The **Qubo Car Dashcam Pro 4K** includes:

- GPS tracking (speed, route history)
- Wi-Fi support for smartphone connectivity
- Easy video access via the Qubo mobile app

### 6. Up to 1TB Storage Support

This dashcam supports microSD cards up to **1TB**, making it ideal for long road trips and continuous recording.

### 7. 2.8-Inch LCD Display

The built-in display allows:

- Live preview
- Instant playback
- Easy settings control

### 8. Built-in Microphone

You can record in-cabin audio along with video, which can be turned off if needed.
