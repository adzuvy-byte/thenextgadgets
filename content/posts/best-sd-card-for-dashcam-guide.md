Choosing the **best SD card for dashcam** isn’t just about storage it’s about durability, write speed, and reliability under continuous recording. Dashcams constantly overwrite footage, which means regular memory cards often fail within months. That’s why experts and real users strongly recommend **high-endurance microSD cards with U3 speed rating** for stable recording and long lifespan.

This guide covers the **best SD cards for dashcam**, including detailed reviews, comparison, and a final recommendation.

## 🧠 What Makes the Best SD Card for Dashcam?

When choosing, focus on:

- **Endurance rating (MOST IMPORTANT)** – built for constant rewriting
- **U3 / V30 speed class** – ensures smooth recording
- **Heat resistance** – essential for Indian summers
- **Storage capacity** – 128GB or higher recommended

👉 Regular SD cards can fail in **3–6 months under dashcam use**, while endurance cards last significantly longer.

If you want the **best SD card for dashcam**, choose:
