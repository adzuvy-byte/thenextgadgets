## How to choose

- **Phone only:** 20W to 30W is enough.
- **Phone and tablet:** 30W to 45W.
- **Laptop:** 65W for thin and light laptops.
- **GaN** chargers are smaller and run cooler at high wattage.

## What to check

- **Port split.** A multi-port charger divides its power between ports.
- **Cable.** Many chargers do not include one.
- **Certification.** Prefer BIS-marked chargers from known sellers.
