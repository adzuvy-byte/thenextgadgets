## What to check

- **Privacy.** WiFi cameras can be hacked. Use a strong password and two-factor sign-in, and place it carefully.
- **Dedicated or WiFi.** A dedicated monitor works without WiFi. A WiFi camera works with your phone.
- **Night vision.** Check for infrared night vision.
- **Two-way audio.** Lets you talk to the baby.
- **Cloud or SD card.** Check how and where video is stored.
- **Not a medical device.** Do not rely on a camera instead of supervision.
