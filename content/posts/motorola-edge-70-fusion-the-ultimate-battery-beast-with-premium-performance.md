## Motorola Edge 70 Fusion Full Specifications

The **Motorola Edge 70 Fusion** delivers a powerful and well-balanced spec sheet:

### Display

The **Motorola Edge 70 Fusion** features a **6.78-inch 1.5K AMOLED quad-curved display** with a **144Hz refresh rate**, delivering ultra-smooth visuals and vibrant colors. It also supports HDR10+ and reaches up to 5200 nits peak brightness.

### Performance

Powered by the **Qualcomm Snapdragon 7s Gen 4 processor**, the Motorola Edge 70 Fusion ensures fast performance for gaming, multitasking, and heavy apps. It comes with up to **12GB RAM and 256GB storage**.

### Battery

The biggest highlight of the **Motorola Edge 70 Fusion** is its **massive 7000mAh silicon-carbon battery**, offering up to **2 days of usage** on a single charge. It supports **68W TurboPower fast charging**, giving hours of usage in minutes.

### Camera

The **Motorola Edge 70 Fusion** offers a premium camera setup:

- 50MP Sony LYTIA 710 main camera with OIS
- 13MP ultra-wide + macro lens
- 32MP front camera

This setup ensures sharp photos, stable videos, and high-quality selfies.

### Audio & Multimedia

With **dual stereo speakers, Dolby Atmos, and Hi-Res audio**, the Motorola Edge 70 Fusion delivers an immersive entertainment experience.

### Build & Durability

The **Motorola Edge 70 Fusion** is built tough with:

- IP68 + IP69 water & dust resistance
- MIL-STD-810H certification
- Gorilla Glass protection

This makes it one of the most durable phones in its segment.

The **Motorola Edge 70 Fusion** runs on Android 16 with **Moto AI 2.0**, offering features like:
