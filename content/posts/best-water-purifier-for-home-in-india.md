## Which purifier suits your water?

- **Borewell or tanker water:** the Pureit Wave Prime is listed for it. RO is the main need when TDS is high.
- **Municipal water:** RO+UV or UF models such as the Aquaguard Delight Aquasaver.
- **Hot water at home:** AO Smith Z9 Pro.
- **Less servicing:** Urban Company Native M1.
- **Lower budget:** Aquaguard Sure Delight.

Test your water TDS before you buy. RO is for high TDS, and UV or UF suits low TDS water. Ask the seller or an RO technician if you are not sure.

## What to check before buying

- **Technology.** RO removes dissolved salts, UV kills microbes, UF is a membrane filter for suspended impurities.
- **Water saving.** RO throws out reject water. Look for a recovery rate on the listing.
- **Tank size.** 7 to 10 litres is common. Families of four or more need the larger ones.
- **Filter and service cost.** Ask what a yearly service and filter set costs.
- **Service in your area.** A purifier is only as good as the nearest technician.
