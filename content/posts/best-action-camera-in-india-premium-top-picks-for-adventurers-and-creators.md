## How to choose

- **Sensor size.** A bigger sensor handles low light better. Several DJI models list a 1/1.3-inch sensor.
- **Stabilisation.** Action cameras differ in how smooth their video looks while moving.
- **Battery and cold.** Check reviews on battery life in the cold.
- **Mounts and combos.** Adventure combos add batteries and mounts. Check what is in the box.
- **360 or standard.** A 360 camera lets you reframe later, but needs more editing.
