## How to choose

- **Size.** 10-inch is fine for phones. 12 to 18 inch gives softer light for portraits.
- **Power.** USB-powered is portable. AC-powered is brighter.
- **Stand height.** Taller stands help standing shots.
- **Colour modes.** Warm and cool lights help match the scene.
- **Remote.** Handy for solo shooting.
