## Product-by-product notes

**Havells Instanio 3L.** The most reviewed geyser on the list. The LED changes from blue to amber as the water heats, which tells you when it is ready without guessing. It has a 304 stainless steel tank, a 5-year tank warranty and a pressure rating suited to pressure pumps and high-rise buildings. It costs more than the budget picks, and for that you get the brand and the review base.

**V-Guard Zio 3L.** A 3 kW unit with a pressure release valve, which is a useful safety feature. The warranty is split: 2 years on the product and element, and 5 years on the tank. A well-reviewed all-rounder at a slightly lower price than the Havells.

**ACTIVA Inferno 3L.** Listed as BEE 5-star and ISI marked, at around ₹1,999 and rated 4.2 by 814 buyers. The low price and the energy rating make it the best value here, but it is a newer model, so check recent reviews on durability.

**ACTIVA 3L 3 kW.** Around ₹1,949 with a copper element, a 304L stainless steel tank and auto cut-off. It has more than 2,200 ratings, which gives some confidence, though ACTIVA is a less established name than Havells or Crompton.

**Longway Hotspring 3L.** The cheapest at around ₹1,799 with an ABS body and multiple safety systems. It has only 18 ratings, so treat the 4.4 score cautiously.

**Sujata 3L.** The tank warranty is 5 years with 2 years on the whole product, but the listing has just 5 ratings. Consider it only if the warranty matters more than proof of track record.

**Crompton Arno Neo 3L.** A 3 kW heater with a food-grade stainless steel tank and a 6 bar pressure rating. Around ₹2,799, rated 4.0 by 912 buyers. The attraction is Crompton's service network.

**Bajaj Prohot 3L.** A vertical 3 kW heater for kitchen or bathroom. Around ₹2,699 and rated 4.1 by 130 buyers, so it has a smaller review base. A reasonable pick if you prefer Bajaj service.


## Kitchen and basin checklist

- **Size.** 3 litres is enough for dishes and hand wash.
- **Orientation.** Vertical models save counter space.
- **Tank.** Stainless steel is preferred.
- **Warranty.** Compare tank and product warranty separately.

## What to check

- **Pressure.** Confirm the bar rating.
- **Installation.** A 3 kW geyser draws about 13 A, so use a 15 or 16 A socket, a suitable MCB and proper earthing. Have an electrician check old wiring first.
