## Kitchen and basin checklist

- **Size.** 3 litres is enough for dishes and hand wash.
- **Orientation.** Vertical models save counter space.
- **Tank.** Stainless steel is preferred.
- **Warranty.** Compare tank and product warranty separately.

## What to check

- **Pressure.** Confirm the bar rating.
- **Installation.** Use a proper socket and earthing.
