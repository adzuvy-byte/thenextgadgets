## How to choose

- **Pocket or interchangeable lens.** A pocket camera is simple. An interchangeable-lens camera is more flexible.
- **Autofocus.** Face and eye tracking make solo shooting easier.
- **Screen.** A flip screen helps you frame yourself.
- **Audio.** Check mic input and wind noise options.
- **Lens.** Wide lenses help for selfie vlogs.
