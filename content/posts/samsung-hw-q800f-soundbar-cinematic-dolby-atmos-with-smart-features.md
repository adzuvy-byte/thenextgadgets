### Overview & Audio Technology

The **Samsung HW-Q800F** is a premium **5.1.2-channel** soundbar released , designed to deliver immersive **Dolby Atmos** and **DTS:X** surround sound without the mess of additional wiring. It includes a wireless subwoofer and supports expansion with Samsung’s SWA‑9500S rear speakers, enabling up to 7.1.4 surround configuration.

### Key Features

- **Dolby Atmos** 3D sound via upward-firing drivers, creating overhead audio effects for cinematic immersion
- **Q‑Symphony & SpaceFit Sound Pro**: automatically syncs soundbar and compatible Samsung TV speakers for enhanced spatial clarity; tunes audio to your room acoustics using built‑in microphones.
- **Game Mode Pro**: synchronizes with TVs for ultra-low latency and directional audio—ideal for gaming.
- **Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, AirPlay, Chromecast Built‑in & Built‑in Voice Assistant** support for seamless streaming and voice control integration.

### Design & Room Size Recommendations

- **Soundbar dimensions**: ~111 cm × 6 cm × 12 cm; **Subwoofer**: ~24 cm cube. Combined weight ~21 kg—solid presence for medium-to-large living rooms.
- Optimized for rooms around **18–30 m²** (~200–320 sq ft), where its 5.1.2 setup fills well without requiring extra rear speakers. Adding SWA-9500S rear units is recommended for larger rooms.

### Performance Highlights

- Exceptional **bass punch and crisp highs**—reviewers note strong performance across movie soundtracks, music and gaming content
- **Consistent with top-tier mids**: praised for clarity and immersive surround, though some concerns were raised about subwoofer build quality or Bluetooth reliability by a few users.

### Samsung HW-Q800F vs JBL Bar 500

- Samsung supports **real Atmos output** and **DTS:X**, delivering a much wider soundstage compared to JBL’s virtual surround only rendering.
- **Samsung also includes smart room calibration (SpaceFit), Q‑Symphony sync**, and built-in voice assistant, giving it a modern feature edge.
- JBL has more raw power and slightly higher RMS output, but the audio experience metrics (mix of clarity, depth, surround) lean toward Samsung overall.

### Samsung HW-Q800F vs LG Q‑Series (Q70TY / S75TR)

- Reviews on Reddit and soundbar forums suggest **Samsung Q-series models outperform LG** in surround immersion and reliability, especially for Atmos content playback.
- LG smart soundbars are frequently criticized for limited app support and inconsistent audio calibration—S75TR is stronger, but still often falls short of Samsung’s seamless ecosystem integration.
