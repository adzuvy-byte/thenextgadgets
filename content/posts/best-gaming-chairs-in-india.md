## How to choose

- **Material.** PU leather looks good but is hot. Fabric is more breathable.
- **Adjustability.** Look for height, recline, armrest and neck support.
- **Gas lift class.** Class 3 and above is common. Class 4 is more durable.
- **Weight limit.** Check it against your body weight.
- **Foam.** Firm foam keeps shape better than soft.
- **Assembly.** Most need DIY assembly. Check what is included.
