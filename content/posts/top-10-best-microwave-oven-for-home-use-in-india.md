## Product-by-product notes

**Godrej 20 L solo (GMX 20SA2).** The cheapest way in here, and the listing carries a 5-year comprehensive warranty, which is rare at this price. It does reheating, defrosting and simple cooking with five power levels. Choose it for a small household that only reheats. The review base is smaller than the Panasonic or IFB, so read recent reviews on service.

**Panasonic 20 L solo (NN-ST26JMFDG).** The most reviewed solo on this list, with 51 auto menus. It suits a couple or a small family that wants a familiar brand with wide service coverage. It cannot grill or bake.

**Haier 20 L solo (HIL2001MFPH).** A compact solo with a 940 W magnetron and a high rating, though from only 73 buyers. The warranty is one year on the product and the magnetron. Reasonable if you find it at a good price, but the better-established brands above have more feedback behind them.

**IFB 24 L solo (24PM2S).** A bigger cavity than the 20 L models, 69 auto menus, mug recipes and a steam clean function that makes cleaning easier. Pick this if you want the highest-rated solo and a little extra space for larger plates.

**Panasonic 20 L grill (NN-GT221WFDG).** A grill model for toast, tikka and paneer, with 800 W power and 38 auto menus. It is a sensible step up from a solo if you do not need to bake. It cannot bake a cake, because it has no convection heating.

**LG 20 L grill (MH2044DB).** A quartz heater grill with Indian cuisine presets. Quartz heaters warm up quickly, which helps for quick snacks. Like the Panasonic grill, it does not bake.

**Whirlpool 20 L convection (Magicook Pro 22CE).** The smallest convection model here, with 118 auto menus and a 5-year magnetron warranty. It can bake, but a 20 L cavity limits the size of cake tins and trays. Best for two or three people who want to bake occasionally.

**Godrej 23 L air fry convection (GME 523 CF1 RM).** Bakes, grills and has an air fry mode, plus a stainless steel cavity and a 5-year comprehensive warranty on the listing. The air fry mode is useful for crisping snacks with little oil, but treat it as a convenience mode and not a replacement for a dedicated air fryer.

**Samsung 28 L convection (MC28A5013AK).** A large cavity for four to six people, a ceramic enamel interior with a 10-year warranty on the cavity, and curd and dough-proof modes. The rest of the unit has a 1-year warranty. Good for families who bake and make curd at home.

**LG 28 L convection (MC2846BV).** Built around Indian cooking, with tandoor, Indian cuisine and steam cook programs and a stainless steel cavity. It has a strong review base of about 1,800 buyers. It is a large unit, so measure your counter before ordering.

**IFB 28 L air fry convection (28SC5).** The best rated of the convection models, with air fry, 300 auto menus, paneer and ghee modes and a ferment mode. It costs the most here. It makes sense only if you will really use the extra modes. If you just reheat, a solo is a better use of money.

## Which type do you need?

- **Solo.** Reheating, defrosting and simple cooking. Cheapest.
- **Grill.** Adds a grill for toast, tikka and paneer.
- **Convection.** Bakes cakes and cooks like an oven. Costs the most.

## What to check

- **Capacity.** 20 L suits one to two people, 23 to 28 L suits a family.
- **Controls.** Mechanical knobs are simple and long-lasting. Digital panels give more presets.
- **Warranty.** Compare product, magnetron and cavity warranties. They differ a lot between brands.
- **Size.** Check your counter space and ventilation.
