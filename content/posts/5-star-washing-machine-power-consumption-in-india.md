The Bureau of Energy Efficiency (BEE) Star Rating is a government-mandated labeling system that helps consumers choose energy-efficient appliances. The star rating is based on the energy consumption of an appliance, with 5 stars being the most energy-efficient.

## Additional Features that Affect Power Consumption

Apart from the star rating, there are some other features that can affect the power consumption of a washing machine. Here are some features to keep in mind when selecting a washing machine:

1. **Inverter Technology:** Inverter technology is becoming more common in washing machines. This technology adjusts the motor speed based on the load, resulting in less power consumption. While this feature may increase the initial cost of the washing machine, it can lead to significant savings in electricity bills in the long run.
2. **Load Detection:** Some washing machines come with load detection sensors that automatically adjust the water and detergent required for a specific load. This feature helps save water and energy by reducing wastage, making it an excellent addition to any washing machine.
3. **Quick Wash:** Quick wash programs are designed to wash clothes in a short period, usually under 30 minutes. While this feature can be convenient, it can also increase the power consumption of the washing machine. It's important to note that quick wash programs may use more electricity and water than regular wash programs.
4. **Drum Size:** The size of the drum in a washing machine can also affect power consumption. Larger drums require more power to operate, which means they consume more electricity. It's important to choose the right size of the drum based on your family's laundry needs.

## Tips to Maximize Energy Efficiency in Washing Machines
