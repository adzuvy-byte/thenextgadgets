## What to check

- **HDMI.** 4K at 30Hz is fine for presentations. 60Hz is smoother for work.
- **PD pass-through.** Lets you charge the MacBook through the hub. 60W is the minimum, 100W is better for MacBook Pro.
- **Ports.** Choose by what you plug in: USB-A, SD card, Ethernet.
- **Heat.** Aluminium hubs warm up, which is normal.
- **Compatibility.** Check the listing for your MacBook model.
