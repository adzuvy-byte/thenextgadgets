## How to choose

- **Room size.** A 75L to 100L cooler suits a large room or hall. Smaller rooms can use a personal cooler instead.
- **Climate.** Coolers work best in dry heat. In humid weather they feel clammy.
- **Tank size.** Bigger tanks run longer between refills.
- **Honeycomb pads.** Thicker pads cool better and last longer.
- **Wheels and weight.** A full cooler is heavy. Castor wheels help.
- **Inverter compatibility.** Handy during power cuts.
