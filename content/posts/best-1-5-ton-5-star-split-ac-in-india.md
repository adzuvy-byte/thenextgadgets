## Check this first: the 2026 BEE rating change

India's Bureau of Energy Efficiency revised its AC star ratings from 1 January 2026. Reports say a 5-star AC rated in 2025 would be downgraded to 4-star under the revised system, and that new 5-star models are about 10% more efficient. An AC labelled 5-star on a listing may therefore carry an older label than one made this year. Check the label year and the ISEER figure on the listing before you buy. ## How to choose a 1.5 ton AC

- **Room size and sun:** 1.5 ton suits roughly 150 to 180 sq ft. Choose a larger unit for top-floor rooms or heavy sun.
- **ISEER and star label:** Higher ISEER means lower running cost. Note the label year given the 2026 change.
- **Inverter compressor:** It varies output to the room temperature, saving power and noise.
- **Noise:** Check the indoor unit decibel figure for bedrooms.
- **Condenser coil and refrigerant:** Copper condenser coils are listed on most models above.
- **Warranty and service:** Check compressor warranty terms and service coverage in your city, and plan an annual service.
