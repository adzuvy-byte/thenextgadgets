## What the extra budget buys you

- **Better drivers.** The OnePlus Buds 4 lists dual drivers with DACs and the realme Buds Air 8 lists 11mm plus 6mm dual dynamic drivers.
- **Hi-Res codecs.** The Oppo Enco Air5 Pro and the realme models list LHDC. It only helps if your phone supports LHDC.
- **Stronger ANC.** The OnePlus Buds 4, Oppo and realme Buds Air 8 list up to 55dB.
- **Call quality.** Six-mic setups are listed on the JBL, Samsung, OnePlus Nord Buds 4 and realme models.
- **Software.** The JBL has a companion app with EQ and Personi-Fi tuning, the boAt has app support, and the Samsung is built for Galaxy phones.
- **Spatial audio.** The boAt Nirvana Ivy lists dynamic head tracking, and the OnePlus and realme models list 3D or 360° spatial audio.
