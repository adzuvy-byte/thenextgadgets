## How to choose

- **Load capacity.** Match it to your heaviest camera or lens. 3 to 5 kg suits most.
- **Height.** Taller tripods help standing shots. Light ones are easier to carry.
- **Head.** A ball head is quick. A 3-way head is precise for video.
- **Material.** Aluminium is cheap, carbon fibre is lighter and costs more.
- **Phone holder.** Most come with one. Check the grip.
