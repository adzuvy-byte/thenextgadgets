## How to choose a wireless microphone

- **Check the connector.** Phones use USB-C or Lightning. iPhones switched to USB-C with the iPhone 15, so older iPhones need a Lightning receiver. Android phones use USB-C.
- **2.4 GHz or Bluetooth.** 2.4 GHz systems use a dedicated receiver and usually have lower delay and a longer range. Bluetooth systems pair directly but may add audio delay.
- **Transmitters.** Two transmitters let you record two speakers at once, useful for interviews.
- **Noise handling.** Noise cancelling helps in busy places, and a windshield (a furry cover) helps outdoors.
- **Battery and charging case.** Check the transmitter battery life and whether a charging case is included.
- **Range.** Listed ranges are measured in open space. Walls and crowds reduce them.
- **Safety recording.** Features like 32-bit float or backup recording help if levels spike.
- **Camera compatibility.** For cameras, check that the receiver connects through the 3.5 mm input or the shoe your camera has.
