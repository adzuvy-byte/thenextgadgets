## Buying Guide - Best DJI Drone for Travel in India

If you're planning to buy a DJI drone for travel in India, there are several factors to consider before making a purchase. Here's a buying guide to help you choose the Best DJI drone for your needs:

1. **Portability:** Since you'll be traveling with the drone, it's important to choose a model that's portable and easy to carry. The DJI Mavic Air 2 and DJI Mini 2 are both lightweight and foldable, making them great options for travel.
2. **Battery life:** You'll want a drone with a good battery life to ensure you have enough time to capture the footage you want. The DJI Mavic Air 2 has a battery life of up to 34 minutes, while the DJI Mini 2 can fly for up to 31 minutes.
3. **Camera quality:**The camera quality is an important factor to consider if you're planning to use the drone for photography or videography. The DJI Mavic Air 2 has a 48MP camera and can shoot 4K video at 60fps, while the DJI Mini 2 has a 12MP camera and can shoot 4K video at 30fps.
4. **Flight features:** DJI drones come with a range of flight features, including obstacle avoidance, active track, and gesture control. Consider which features are important to you and choose a drone that has them.
5. **Price:** DJI drones can range in price from a few hundred to several thousand dollars. Consider your budget and choose a drone that meets your needs without breaking the bank.

Based on these factors, the DJI Mavic Air 2 and DJI Mini 2 are both great options for travel in India. They are both portable, have good battery life, and high-quality cameras. The Mavic Air 2 has more advanced features, while the Mini 2 is a more affordable option.
