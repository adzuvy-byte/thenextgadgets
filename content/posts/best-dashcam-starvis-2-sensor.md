## What STARVIS 2 means

It is a Sony sensor family built for low light. It does not guarantee a great picture, since the lens, processing and mounting matter too.

## What to check

- **Channels.** Front only, dual, or three channel with a cabin camera.
- **Resolution.** Compare native resolution, not upscaled claims.
- **Price.** STARVIS 2 models start near ₹4,400 and go up quickly.
- **Warranty.** Check the brand's service in your city.
