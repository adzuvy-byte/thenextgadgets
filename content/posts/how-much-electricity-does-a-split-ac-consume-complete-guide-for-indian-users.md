## How Much Electricity Does a Split AC Consume?

The electricity consumption of a split AC depends on:

- AC capacity (1 Ton, 1.5 Ton, 2 Ton)
- Star rating (3 Star vs 5 Star)
- Usage hours
- Inverter vs non-inverter technology

### Average Power Consumption (Per Hour)

| AC Capacity | Power Consumption (Approx) |
|---|---|
| 1 Ton AC | 0.8 – 1.2 units/hour |
| 1.5 Ton AC | 1.2 – 1.8 units/hour |
| 2 Ton AC | 1.8 – 2.5 units/hour |

👉 1 unit = 1 kWh (kilowatt-hour)

## Monthly Electricity Consumption Example

Let’s calculate for a **1.5 Ton split AC**:

- Usage: 8 hours/day
- Consumption: ~1.5 units/hour

**Daily Consumption:** 1.5 × 8 = 12 units

**Monthly Consumption (30 days):** 12 × 30 = **360 units**

## Electricity Cost Calculation in India

Electricity cost varies by state and by slab. As an example, we use ₹8 per unit. Check your own bill for your actual rate.

### Example:

- 360 units × ₹8 = **₹2,880 per month**

👉 So, running a 1.5 Ton split AC can cost around **₹2,500–₹3,500/month** depending on usage.

## Inverter vs Non-Inverter AC Consumption

### Inverter Split AC

- Adjusts compressor speed
- Consumes less electricity
- Saves **20–40% power**

### Non-Inverter AC

- Compressor turns ON/OFF repeatedly
- Higher power consumption

👉 If you use AC daily, inverter AC is more economical.

## 3 Star vs 5 Star AC Power Consumption

| Star Rating | Power Usage | Savings |
|---|---|---|
| 3 Star AC | Higher consumption | Lower upfront cost |
| 5 Star AC | Lower consumption | Saves ₹1,000–₹2,000/year |

👉 A **5 Star split AC** is ideal for long-term savings.

## How AC Size Affects Electricity Consumption

Choosing the wrong size increases power usage:

- Small room + big AC → unnecessary power usage
- Large room + small AC → runs continuously → more units

### Room Size Guide:

- Up to 120 sq ft → 1 Ton
- 120–180 sq ft → 1.5 Ton
- 180–300 sq ft → 2 Ton

## Factors That Increase AC Electricity Consumption

- Poor insulation (heat entering room)
- Direct sunlight exposure
- Dirty filters or coils
- Low-quality installation
- Frequent door opening

## Tips to Reduce Split AC Electricity Bill

### 1. Set Ideal Temperature (24–26°C)

Every 1°C lower can increase power usage by ~6%.

### 2. Clean Filters Regularly

Dirty filters reduce efficiency and increase consumption.

### 3. Close Doors & Windows

Prevents cool air from escaping.

### 4. Use Ceiling Fan with AC

Helps circulate cool air and reduces load.

### 5. Use Timer & Sleep Mode

Avoid unnecessary usage during sleep.

### 6. Block Sunlight

Use curtains or blinds during the day.

## Quick Summary

- 1 Ton AC → ~1 unit/hour
- 1.5 Ton AC → ~1.5 units/hour
- 2 Ton AC → ~2 units/hour
- Monthly cost → ₹2,000 to ₹4,000 (depending on usage)

## Final Thoughts

So, **how much electricity does a split AC consume?** It depends on tonnage, usage, and efficiency—but on average, a typical Indian household spends **₹2,500–₹3,500/month** running a split AC during peak summer.

Choosing the right AC, using it efficiently, and maintaining it properly can significantly reduce your electricity bills.
