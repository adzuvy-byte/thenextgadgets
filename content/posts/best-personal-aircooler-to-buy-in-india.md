## How to choose

- **Room size.** Personal coolers suit small rooms, up to a bedroom. For larger spaces see our desert cooler list.
- **Tank size.** Larger tanks run longer before refilling.
- **Air throw.** Check the distance in feet.
- **Noise.** Fans can be loud on high.
- **Humidity.** Coolers are for dry heat.
