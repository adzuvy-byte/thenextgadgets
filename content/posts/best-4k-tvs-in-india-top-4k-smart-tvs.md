## Which 4K TV should you choose?

- **Gaming:** the TCL 55Q6C (144Hz) or Xiaomi X Pro (120Hz game booster).
- **Sound:** the Hisense 55E75Q (48W, Dolby Atmos) or TCL (46W Onkyo 2.1).
- **Long-term software support:** the Samsung Mini LED.

## How to choose a 4K TV

- **Panel type.** Mini LED gives better contrast than standard QLED or LED, at a higher price.
- **Refresh rate.** 60Hz is fine for TV and films; 120Hz or more helps gaming.
- **HDR.** Dolby Vision and HDR10+ matter for streaming; check what your model supports.
- **Operating system.** Google TV has the widest app choice; webOS, Tizen and VIDAA have their own stores.
- **Warranty.** Check panel warranty terms and local service.
