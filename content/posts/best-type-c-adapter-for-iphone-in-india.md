## Which one should you buy?

- **Just an iPhone:** the Spigen 45W is plenty and the smallest option here.
- **iPhone plus a laptop or iPad:** a 65W charger. The Belkin lists a 2-year warranty and splits its output (45W + 20W) across two ports. The Spigen 65W lists PD and PPS support.
- **Several devices at once:** the 70W Ambrane has three ports, and the Spigen 70W has two.
- **Always:** buy a BIS-certified charger and a good cable. Our [USB-C cable guide](/top-10-best-usb-type-c-to-usb-type-c-cables-in-india/) covers cables, and the [GaN charger guide](/best-gan-charger/) covers higher-watt options.

## What to look for

- **Power Delivery (PD).** This is the fast-charging standard iPhones use, so make sure the charger supports it.
- **GaN.** Smaller and cooler than older chargers at the same wattage.
- **Ports.** A second port lets you charge a phone and earbuds or a watch together.
- **Safety.** Look for BIS certification and a recognised brand.
- **Cable.** An iPhone with a USB-C port needs a USB-C to USB-C cable; older iPhones need USB-C to Lightning.
