## How to choose

- **Size.** 20L suits one to three people. 25L suits a family and fits a cake tin.
- **Wattage.** 1300 to 1700W. Higher heats faster.
- **Heating modes.** More modes (top, bottom, both, fan) give you control.
- **Rotisserie.** Useful for tandoor-style chicken.
- **Safety.** Cool-touch handles and a timer help.
