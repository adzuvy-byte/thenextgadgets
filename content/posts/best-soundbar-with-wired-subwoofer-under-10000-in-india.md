## What to check

- **How the sub connects.** Wired subs need a cable run. Wireless ones need only power.
- **Channels.** 2.1 is enough for TV. 5.1 adds rear speakers.
- **Watts.** Manufacturer wattage claims are peak, not real loudness.
- **Ports.** HDMI ARC is best, optical works too.
- **Room size.** Match the system to the room.
