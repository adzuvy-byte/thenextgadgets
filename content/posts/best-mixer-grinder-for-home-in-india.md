## How to choose

- **Power.** 500W is fine for a small family. Go to 750W or more if you grind a lot of wet batter or hard masalas.
- **Jars.** Stainless steel jars last. Look for a chutney jar and a dry grinding jar.
- **Warranty.** Check the product warranty and the motor warranty, which can be different.
- **Overload protection.** A cut-off protects the motor if you overfill.
- **Noise.** Mixers are loud. Read recent reviews on noise.
