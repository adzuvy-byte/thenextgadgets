## Comparison: 70mai vs Qubo

They offer a variety of features and price points, so it can be tough to decide which one is right for you. Here's a comparison of the two brands to help you make a decision:

1. **Video Quality**: Both 70mai and Qubo dashcams generally provide good video quality. However, specific models within each brand may differ in terms of resolution and clarity. It's important to compare the resolution (e.g., 1080p, 1440p, or 4K) and image sensor quality to determine which one suits your needs.
2. **Field of View (FOV)**: The FOV determines how wide an area the dashcam can capture. Both 70mai and Qubo dashcams have different models with varying FOV. Consider whether you prefer a wider or narrower field of view based on your specific requirements.
3. **Features and Functions**: Both brands offer a range of features such as loop recording, G-sensor for impact detection, parking mode, built-in Wi-Fi, mobile app connectivity, and more. It's advisable to compare the specific features of the models you are interested in to see which align with your preferences.
4. **Design and Installation**: Dashcams from both 70mai and Qubo come in compact designs that are easy to install on your vehicle's windshield. Consider factors such as the size, shape, and ease of installation to determine which one suits your requirements.
5. **Price**: Pricing can vary depending on the specific model and features offered by each brand. Compare the prices of the dashcams within your budget and consider the value you're getting for the price.
6. **Customer Reviews and Ratings**: Reading reviews and ratings from other users can provide insights into the performance and reliability of the dashcams. Look for feedback on video quality, durability, ease of use, and customer support to help inform your decision.

## Buying Guide

## Things to Consider when choosing a Dashcam

Some things to consider when choosing a dashcam:

- **Resolution:** The higher the resolution, the better the video quality will be. However, higher resolution also means that the dashcam will require more storage space.
- **Field of view:** The wider the field of view, the more of the road will be captured in the video.
- **Night vision:** Good night vision is important if you drive at night.
- **Parking mode:** Parking mode allows the dashcam to record even when your car is parked. This can be helpful if your car is hit while it is parked.
- **GPS:** GPS can be helpful for recording your speed and location.
- **Wi-Fi:** Wi-Fi makes it easy to transfer videos from the dashcam to your phone or computer.
- **Battery:** The battery life of the dashcam is important if you plan to use it without connecting it to a power source.
- **Storage:** The dashcam will need to store the videos it records. Some dashcams have built-in storage, while others require you to use a microSD card.
- **Price:** Dashcams can range in price from a few hundred rupees to a few thousand rupees. Choose a dashcam that fits your budget and your needs.
