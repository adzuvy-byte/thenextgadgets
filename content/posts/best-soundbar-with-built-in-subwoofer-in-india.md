## What to know

- **Bass from a built-in sub is limited.** A separate wireless sub gives deeper bass. A built-in sub is simpler.
- **Channels.** 2.0 and 2.1 are enough for most TVs. 3.1 adds a centre channel for dialogue.
- **Connections.** HDMI ARC is best. Optical and Bluetooth are fallbacks.
- **Size.** Check it fits below your TV.
