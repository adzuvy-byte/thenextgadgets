## The short answer

- **Choose Ulanzi** if you want compact, travel-friendly and modular gear for phones, mirrorless cameras and vlogging rigs.
- **Choose Digitek** if you want a low-cost, easy-to-find tripod, with Indian support and a wide range of sizes, for everyday phone and basic camera work.
- For heavy cameras and long lenses, check the load rating on any model and consider a sturdier tripod from either brand.

## What to compare on any tripod

- **Maximum load.** Make sure it is comfortably above the weight of your camera, lens and accessories.
- **Maximum and folded height.** Folded size matters for travel, and maximum height for standing shots.
- **Leg locks and stability.** Twist or flip locks should hold firmly and not slip.
- **Head and mount.** A ball head is fast. A quick-release plate speeds up swaps between camera and phone.
- **Material and weight.** Aluminium is common at this price. A lighter tripod is easier to carry but can be less stable in wind.
- **Phone holder.** The clamp should grip without slipping.
- **Reviews.** Read recent reviews for build quality and defects.

## Buying tips for India

- Buy from an authorised seller or the brand's official store on Amazon.in, so the warranty works.
- Match the tripod to your heaviest setup, not your current phone.
- Test the leg locks and head as soon as the tripod arrives, and return it if anything wobbles.
- Look at what is included, such as a phone clamp, remote and carry bag.
