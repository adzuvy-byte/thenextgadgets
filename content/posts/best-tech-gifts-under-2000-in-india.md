## How we chose

- **Daily use.** If it is not used weekly, it is clutter.
- **Easy to start.** No account, hub or tutorial should be needed for the basic job.
- **Honest limits.** Each pick below says who should skip it.
- **A sensible review base.** We looked at rating and number of reviews together, not rating alone, since a 4.5 from forty buyers means little.

## Notes on each pick

**The mouse.** A mouse is a quiet upgrade for anyone who works on a laptop trackpad all day. Between the two, the Portronics has far more buyer feedback. The Amkette is the one to pick if you dislike changing batteries.

**The power bank.** We were careful here. A 5000 mAh bank does not fully recharge a modern phone, since phone batteries are commonly in the 4,000 to 5,000 mAh range and charging loses some energy. Give it to someone who wants a safety net, not as the only charger for a trek.

**The smart plug.** It can switch an appliance on a schedule and show how much power it uses. Check the plug's rated load against the appliance first. For heavy loads like a geyser, follow the maker's instructions and never use a plug to switch something above its rating. If you are unsure, skip this gift.

**The kettle.** Boiling water on a gas stove is slow and wasteful for one cup. A small kettle is quick. Look for an auto cut-off and keep it away from the edge of the counter.

- Ask yourself: will this be used in a month? If not, pick something else.
