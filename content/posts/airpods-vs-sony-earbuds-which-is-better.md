Apple's AirPods Pro 3 (launched September 2025) and Sony's WF-1000XM6 (launched February 2026) are the two flagship earbuds most people compare. They look similar on paper, since both are rated for 8 hours of listening with noise cancelling on and 24 hours with the case. They are different in what they are good at. ## The short answer

- **Choose AirPods Pro 3** if you use an iPhone and want the best noise cancelling, the easiest setup and the most features.
- **Choose Sony WF-1000XM6** if sound quality is your first priority, or if you use an Android phone.
- **Want to spend less?** The previous Sony WF-1000XM5 and AirPods Pro 2 are often discounted, and TechRadar suggests the XM5 as a value alternative to the XM6.

## Noise cancelling: AirPods win

TechRadar calls the AirPods Pro 3 noise cancelling "remarkable". The Sony XM6 is better than earlier Sony earbuds, but the reviewer describes it as still not elite, and says fit issues can hurt it. If you commute by train or fly often, this matters. Getting a proper seal with the right ear tip helps on both pairs, and Sony includes foam tips that some people find more comfortable.

## Sound quality: Sony wins

The XM6 gives more detail, more lifelike bass and a wider, more precise soundstage. The review says it sounds better even over AAC, which is the codec an iPhone uses, so you do not need LDAC to hear the difference. Sony also lets you adjust the EQ in its app, while the AirPods have fewer controls.

## Features and ecosystem

- **On iPhone:** AirPods connect instantly, switch between your Apple devices, use Find My and add heart-rate tracking and hearing health features that work only with Apple devices.
- **On Android:** Most of those extras do not work. The Sony offers LE Audio, Auracast broadcasting, multipoint for two devices at once and spatial audio on Android.
- **Water resistance:** AirPods Pro 3 carry IP57 on the buds and the case. The Sony's IPX4 rating covers the buds only, so it is fine for sweat and light rain but not for heavy exposure.
- **Case and comfort:** the AirPods case is smaller and more pocketable, and the buds are lighter. Sony's buds are easier to grip.
