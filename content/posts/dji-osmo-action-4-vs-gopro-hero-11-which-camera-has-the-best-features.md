## The short answer

The Osmo Action 6 is the better pick for most people on features, video quality and battery. The GoPro Hero 13 Black still makes sense if you want GoPro's accessory ecosystem or its anamorphic lens option.

## Side-by-side

| | DJI Osmo Action 6 | GoPro Hero 13 Black |
|---|---|---|
| Max video | 8K at 30 fps; 4K up to 120 fps | 5.7K at 30 fps |
| Waterproof without a case | 20 m (65 ft) | 10 m (33 ft) |
| Battery stress test | About 120 min | About 80 min |

## Video and stabilisation

The Action 6 is the first Osmo Action to shoot 8K, and Tom's Guide praises its video quality, RockSteady 3.0 stabilisation and internal audio. It also offers D-Log M and colour filters. The Hero 13 tops out at 5.7K. Honestly, most people will publish at 4K or 1080p, so the extra resolution matters less than the stabilisation and low-light results.

## Battery and durability

DJI's waterproofing, battery life and cold-weather rating all lead. That makes the Action 6 the safer choice for diving, snow, long rides and mountain trips. It also keeps compatibility with existing DJI mounts and batteries, though finger-mount accessories still need an adapter.

## Where the GoPro still wins

- **Anamorphic lens support.** The Hero 13 supports an anamorphic lens, and the Action 6 does not.
- **Accessories.** GoPro has a huge range of mounts, grips and third-party accessories.
- **Familiar app and workflow** for existing GoPro users.

## Downsides of the Action 6

- Subject tracking works at 2.7K only, and there is no 6K at 60 fps step between 4K/60 and 8K/30.
- It overheated in the reviewer's stress test, though that is less of a concern outdoors.
- Lens flare is visible at night with bright light sources.
- DJI has pulled official support in the US. That does not apply in India, but check the warranty terms from your seller.

## What about the Action 4 and Hero 11?

Both are still capable, and often cheaper. If you shoot mostly in daylight at 4K and do not need the best waterproofing or battery, they remain good value. Before buying, confirm that you are getting a new unit from an authorised seller.
