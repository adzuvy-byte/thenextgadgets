## 15 L or 25 L?

- **15 litres:** one or two people, or a second bathroom. It heats faster and uses less power per heating cycle.
- **25 litres:** a family of three or four showering in turn. It gives more hot water before you wait for the tank to recover.
- **Bigger families or long showers:** consider 35 litres or more, or a second geyser. We have not covered those sizes here.

## How to choose a storage geyser

- **Star rating.** A 5-star BEE rating means lower standby power use. Most models here list five stars. The AO Smith VAS Plus 25 L and the Racold list four.
- **Hard water.** If your water is hard, look for an anti-scale or glass-lined tank. The Crompton Arno Prime lists protection up to 2000 ppm, and the Havells Monza Pro and Instanio Prime are listed as engineered for hard water.
- **High-rise pressure.** Several list high-rise compatibility. Check that the pressure rating suits your building.
- **Warranty.** Compare tank, element and product warranties separately. The Crompton Arno Prime lists 8 years on the tank. The Bajaj lists 7 years on the tank and 6 on the element.
- **Installation.** Some listings include free installation or pipes. Check what is in the box.
- **Safety.** Look for a thermostat, a pressure relief valve and a proper earthed connection.
