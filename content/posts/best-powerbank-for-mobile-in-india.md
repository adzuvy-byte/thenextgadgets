## What to check before buying

- **Capacity (mAh).** A 20,000mAh bank is labelled for the cell capacity. You get less than that at the phone, so expect fewer full charges than the number suggests.
- **Wattage.** Higher watts only help if your phone supports fast charging.
- **Ports and cables.** Built-in cables are handy but cannot be replaced. Check what your device needs.
- **Flying.** Power banks go in cabin baggage, not in checked baggage. Check your airline's limits for large capacities.
- **Warranty.** Check the seller's terms on the listing.
