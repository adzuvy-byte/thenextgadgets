## Check your laptop first

Before you order, check what your laptop takes.

- **Slot type.** Open the laptop manual or the maker's spec page. Does it have an M.2 slot, or only a 2.5-inch SATA bay?
- **Size.** Most laptops take M.2 2280. Some thin ones use 2230, which is a different size.
- **PCIe version.** A Gen 4 drive works in a Gen 3 slot, but at Gen 3 speed.
- **Heat.** Very fast drives get hot, and thin laptops may slow them down.
- **Cloning.** Plan how you will move your data. A cloning tool or a USB enclosure helps.
- **Warranty.** Several listings show 5 years. Read the seller's terms.
