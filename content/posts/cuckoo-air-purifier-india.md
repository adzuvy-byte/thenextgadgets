A Cuckoo air purifier is not the first name most Indian buyers think of. The Korean brand is better known for water purifiers, but its **Respure** has quietly built a following on Amazon.in. It costs ₹13,990, is rated 4.4 out of 5 (707 ratings shared across Cuckoo's Amazon.in listings), and claims a CADR of 408 m³/h for rooms up to 570 sq ft. This guide covers what the Respure actually offers, where the claims need care, filter and running costs, and who should pick it over a Coway, Qubo or Philips. Everything below was checked on the Amazon.in listing and the manufacturer's manual on 11 Nov 2026.

## What is good about it

**Strong airflow for the price.** A CADR of 408 m³/h at ₹13,990 is competitive. By the usual rule of multiplying room volume in cubic metres by five, it suits a room of about 80 cubic metres, which is roughly 280 sq ft at a 10 ft ceiling for full five-air-change cleaning, or larger rooms at a lower rate.

**An affordable replacement filter.** At ₹2,990 the replacement is cheaper than a Philips FY1700/30 (₹4,995) and close to a Coway Airmega 150 filter (₹3,299). Over three years with two changes, the cost is about ₹13,990 + ₹5,980 = ₹19,970. If you replace filters every six months in smoggy cities, double the filter spend.

**A simple, honest control set.** Sleep, Auto and Turbo modes, a timer (1, 4 or 8 hours) and a colour air quality light. There is nothing to configure.

**Compact and light.** At 6 kg it is easy to move between rooms.

## Where to be careful

- **Noise.** The manual gives 57 dB at maximum, which is clearly audible and closer to normal conversation. The Amazon listing quotes a lower low-speed figure. Use Sleep mode at night and keep Turbo for when you are out.
- **Coverage claim.** "570 sq ft in 15 minutes" is a best-case figure. Plan on about 250 to 350 sq ft for real winter smog.
- **No obvious app.** Neither the manual nor the listing mentions Wi-Fi or an app, so we are treating it as an app-free purifier. Confirm on the listing before you buy if you need smart control.
- **Carbon layer.** The manual describes a "deodorisation filter", and the listing does not clearly describe carbon. It will help with odours, but do not expect a dedicated chemical-removal filter.
- **Small review base for filters.** The replacement filter has 21 reviews, so there is little long-term evidence yet.
- **Service in India.** We did not find a published list of Cuckoo air purifier service centres. Ask the seller how warranty claims work, especially outside a metro.

## Who should buy the Cuckoo Respure?

**Buy it if:**
- You want a high-CADR purifier under ₹15,000 with cheap filters.
- You do not need an app.
- Your room is up to about 350 sq ft.

**Skip it if:**
- You need very low noise at full speed.
- You want Wi-Fi and voice control.
- You want a long-established service network.

## How to get the most from it

1. Place it at least 20 cm from walls or furniture, as the manual advises.
2. Clean the washable pre-filter every two to four weeks.
3. Reset the filter indicator after replacement by holding the filter button.
4. Keep doors and windows closed while it runs.
5. Clean the air quality sensor periodically, as dust on it skews the readings.
