## Which size should you buy?

- **1 to 2 people:** 3.5 to 4 litres. The Inalsa NutriFry 3.5 or the Kent Digi Plus.
- **3 to 4 people:** 4.2 litres. The Havells (see-through window and 2-year warranty) is the best listed warranty here, and the Philips NA120 is the safe brand pick.
- **5 or more people, or batch cooking:** 6.2 litres. The Philips NA231 if you want touch controls, the NA130 otherwise.
- **Cooking more than frying:** the Kent lists grill, roast and bake functions too.

## How to choose an air fryer

- **Capacity.** Plan roughly one litre per person for meals, a bit more for chips and wings.
- **Wattage.** Higher wattage preheats faster. Wattage is in some titles and not others, so check the page.
- **Controls.** Digital and touch panels are easier to set; check temperature and time ranges.
- **Basket coating.** Look for a non-stick basket that is dishwasher safe and easy to clean.
- **Warranty.** Compare the warranty and the service network in your city.
