The DJI Air 3 launched in 2023 as the drone that finally put two cameras in the mid-size Air line. DJI has since released the Air 3S, which is better in low light and adds forward LiDAR. So the real question now is whether the older Air 3 is still the smart buy, usually because it costs less. This guide covers what the Air 3 does well, where the Air 3S pulls ahead, and who each drone suits. Comparison details for the Air 3S come from DroneXL's Air 3 vs Air 3S comparison, and we do not quote Indian prices, which change often. Check the current price and stock on Amazon.in or DJI's authorised sellers.

## DJI Air 3 at a glance

- **Cameras:** a wide camera and a 70 mm medium-tele camera, both on 1/1.3-inch sensors, so you can switch between a wide scene and a compressed, closer look without moving the drone.
- **Video:** up to 4K at 100 fps for slow motion.
- **Weight:** about 720 g, which puts it in the foldable, travel-friendly class.
- **Transmission:** DJI's O4 system.
- **Obstacle sensing:** omnidirectional, using six camera-based sensors.
- **Flight time:** DJI rates the Air 3 for around 46 minutes in ideal conditions. Expect less in real use, especially in wind.

## What the Air 3 does well

- **Two focal lengths in one drone.** The medium-tele lens is the Air 3's best feature. It flattens landscapes, isolates subjects and keeps you farther from them, which helps on safer shoots.
- **Long battery life.** One battery gives time to scout a location and then shoot, which matters for travel.
- **Portable.** It folds small enough for a backpack.
- **Good daylight image quality.** In good light, the gap to the Air 3S narrows considerably, according to DroneXL.

## Where the Air 3S is better

| | DJI Air 3 | DJI Air 3S |
|---|---|---|
| Weight | 720 g | 724 g |
| Main camera | 1/1.3-inch, wide | 1-inch, 24 mm f/1.8 |
| Telephoto | 1/1.3-inch, f/2.8, 70 mm | 1/1.3-inch, f/2.8, 70 mm (better processing) |
| Max slow motion | 4K up to 100 fps | 4K up to 120 fps |
| Forward LiDAR | No | Yes |
| Camera matching | The two cameras do not fully match | Same frame rates, resolutions and colour profiles |

The Air 3S also handles night and dusk flying better, and its forward LiDAR adds a safety margin.

## Who should buy the Air 3

- You mostly fly in good daylight and want two lenses.
- You want a long-flying travel drone and the price difference to the Air 3S matters.
- You are upgrading from a smaller Mini drone and want a step up.

## Who should buy the Air 3S instead

- You shoot at sunrise, sunset or at night.
- You want the larger sensor and the added LiDAR safety.
- You plan to keep the drone for several years.

## Before you fly in India

- **Know the rules.** Drone flying in India is regulated. Check the current rules, the airspace map and registration requirements before your first flight. Many places, such as airports, are restricted.
- **Do not fly over crowds or private property** without permission.
- **Carry spare batteries and a charger** and follow airline battery rules when you travel.
- **Buy from an authorised seller** so the warranty is valid.
