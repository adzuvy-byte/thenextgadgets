## Echo Dot or Echo Spot?

- **Echo Dot (5th Gen):** pick it for a living room, kitchen or study. The motion detection and temperature sensor let routines switch lights or fans for you.
- **Echo Spot:** pick it for a bedroom. It is designed as a smart alarm clock.

## What to check before buying a smart speaker

- **Which assistant you want.** These are Alexa devices. If you use Google Assistant at home, look at Google speakers instead.
- **Smart home support.** Check that your lights, plugs and TV work with Alexa.
- **Privacy.** Smart speakers listen for a wake word. You can mute the microphone and review voice history in the Alexa app.
- **Sound.** Both are small, so they suit casual listening, not a party. For music, pair them with a larger Bluetooth speaker. See our [portable Bluetooth speaker guide](/best-wireless-portable-bluetooth-speakers-under-5000-in-india/).
- **Wi-Fi.** A smart speaker needs a working internet connection to respond.
