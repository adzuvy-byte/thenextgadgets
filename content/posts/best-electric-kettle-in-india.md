## How to choose an electric kettle

- **Body.** Stainless steel lasts and does not break. Glass shows the water level. Prefer a steel inner body in either case.
- **Capacity.** 1 to 1.2L for one or two cups, 1.5 to 1.8L for a family.
- **Power.** More watts boil faster but draw more load. 1200 to 1500W is common, 2200W is fast.
- **Safety.** Look for auto shut-off and dry-boil protection.
- **Temperature control.** Useful for green tea, coffee and baby formula. Check the range and accuracy.
- **Warranty.** Compare warranty length and service in your city.
