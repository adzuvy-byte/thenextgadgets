## DSLR or mirrorless?

DSLRs are no longer being developed by most makers. Mirrorless cameras are smaller and have better video and autofocus. A DSLR still has strengths: battery life and a big lens used market.

## What to check

- **Sensor.** APS-C is the common size at this price.
- **Lens.** Kit lenses are a start. Budget for a prime later.
- **Video.** Check 4K and mic input.
- **Lens mount.** Canon RF, Sony E and Nikon F each have different lens costs.
