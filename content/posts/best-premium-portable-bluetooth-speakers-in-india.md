## Who should pay more?

- **JBL Flip 7:** for people who want a balanced, rugged speaker (IP68) with long playtime (16 hours), an app and Auracast to link several speakers.
- **Marshall Willen II:** for people who want a design-led speaker (Black and Brass finish) with an IP67 rating and 17 or more hours of playtime.

## What the extra money buys

- **Build and ratings.** Both list IP67 or IP68, so they handle dust, rain and a pool-side splash.
- **Battery.** Both list 16 to 17+ hours, which is more than the pocket-sized speakers in our cheaper guide.
- **Features.** The JBL adds AI Sound Boost, Auracast and a personalisation app.
- **Looks.** The Marshall is as much a design object as a speaker.
- **What it does not buy.** A higher price does not guarantee louder or fuller sound than a good mid-priced speaker. If you want a loud party speaker, check reviews for the specific model.
