## How to choose an oil-filled heater

- **Fins and wattage.** More fins mean a larger surface to release heat. A 2900 W heater suits a room of roughly 200 to 250 sq ft, while 2500 W suits somewhat less. These are rough guides that depend on insulation and the climate.
- **Check the socket.** 2900 W draws about 12.6 A at 230 V. Use a socket and wiring rated for it, and avoid light extension boards.
- **Thermostat.** The thermostat lets the heater cycle on and off, which saves power and keeps temperature steady. Check that the model you buy has one.
- **Fan or no fan.** A fan warms the room faster but adds noise. Without one the heater is silent but slow.
- **Safety.** Look for tip-over and overheat protection. Keep the heater upright and clear of curtains and bedding. Never dry clothes on it.
- **Running cost.** A 2900 W heater uses up to 2.9 units per hour at full power. The thermostat means it will draw less on average.
