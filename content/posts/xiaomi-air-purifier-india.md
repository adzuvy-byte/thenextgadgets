Xiaomi sells air purifiers in India under two names: the older **Xiaomi** branding (Smart Air Purifier 4 Lite) and the newer **Mijia by Xiaomi** line launched in August 2026 (Air Purifier 4 Compact and Smart Air Purifier 6). That naming alone confuses buyers, and the headline coverage figures on the listings are generous. This guide sorts out the current models, what each is really suited to, filter costs and which one to pick. Prices and ratings were checked on Amazon.in on 11 Nov 2026, and launch details come from coverage of Xiaomi India's announcement.

## Xiaomi vs Mijia: what is the difference?

Mijia is Xiaomi's smart-home product brand. In India two of the three purifiers carry the Mijia name and the 4 Lite keeps Xiaomi's. All three use the Xiaomi Home app, which handles schedules, timers, filter checks and remote control. We found no practical difference in support between the two names, but check that the seller on Amazon.in is the official store.

## Coverage claims: read them carefully

The listing for the Mijia 4 Compact says "covers 400 sq ft", yet its own bullet states that it purifies spaces of 172 to 291 sq ft, or up to 517 sq ft on a 2-air-change-per-hour basis. The 4 Lite listing shows both 462 and 640 sq ft in different places. The Purifier 6 is billed as covering up to 940 sq ft on two air changes per hour.

For real winter smog, we advise:

- **Mijia 4 Compact:** a bedroom or study of up to about 170 to 200 sq ft.
- **Xiaomi 4 Lite:** a room of 250 to 350 sq ft. Xiaomi's own range for it is 269 to 463 sq ft.
- **Mijia Purifier 6:** a living room of about 300 to 500 sq ft.

## Model by model

### Mijia Air Purifier 4 Compact (₹6,990)

A small, light (2.2 kg) purifier with a three-in-one filter and a PM2.5 sensor. It claims TÜV Rheinland Allergy Care certification, a particle CADR of 230 m³/h and 20 dB in sleep mode with the display off. It draws up to 27 W. The Xiaomi Home app, Alexa and Google Assistant are supported. It is the cheapest app-connected purifier from a major brand we can recommend for a small room.

### Xiaomi Smart Air Purifier 4 Lite (₹10,990)

The best-known Xiaomi purifier in India. Three layers: primary filter, high-efficiency filter and activated carbon. CADR is 360 m³/h on the listing, and noise in night mode is about 33 dB. It also carries TÜV Rheinland Allergy Care certification. Compatible third-party filters are about ₹2,400 to ₹3,000 on Amazon.in. We did not find an official replacement filter price on Xiaomi's pages, so check at purchase.

### Mijia Smart Air Purifier 6 (₹17,999)

The top model. Particle CADR is 443 m³/h and formaldehyde CADR 218 m³/h, with five stages: primary, high-efficiency, activated carbon, UV-C sterilisation and negative ions. It shows PM2.5, PM1, temperature and humidity on a colour display, runs at about 32 dB in sleep mode, and supports Alexa and Google Assistant. UV-C and ionisers add features but not necessarily cleaner air, so do not pay extra for them alone. Because the formaldehyde CADR is published, it suits homes with new paint or furniture.

Before buying, message the Amazon seller or Xiaomi support for the genuine price and availability. Third-party filters are cheaper but unverified, and a poorly fitted filter lets air bypass the HEPA layer.
