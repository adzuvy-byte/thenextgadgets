## What a bigger battery does not tell you

- **Charging speed.** Only the Moto g77 Power lists a charging speed (30W). For the others, check the in-box charger on the listing.
- **Screen and chip.** A big cell with a weak chip or a sharp screen can still drain fast. Check the processor and resolution too.
- **5G bands.** All five are 5G, but band support varies. Check that the phone supports the bands your network uses.
- **Weight.** Bigger batteries add weight and thickness. Look at the listing dimensions if you care.
- **Software support.** A big battery on an unsupported phone is a short-term buy. The Galaxy M17e is the one that lists a long OS upgrade promise.
