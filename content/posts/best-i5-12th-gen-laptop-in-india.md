## How to choose

- **13th Gen vs Core 5.** Both are current. Core i5-13420H is an H-series chip, so expect more performance. The U-series chips (1334U, 1335U, Core 5 120U) favour battery life.
- **RAM.** 16GB is more comfortable than 8GB for multitasking.
- **Storage.** 512GB SSD is a good minimum.
- **Reviews.** Several 2026 models have only a handful of reviews. Read recent ones.

## What to check

- **Windows and Office.** Some listings include Office, some do not.
- **Display.** Look for FHD IPS and 250 nits or more.
- **Weight and battery.** The listing page has both.
- **Warranty and service.** Check what is covered in your city.
