## How to choose a neckband

- **ANC.** Three of these four list active noise cancellation. The realme lists 50dB, the OnePlus 45dB hybrid and the boAt about 32dB. A higher number usually means stronger cancelling, but real performance varies.
- **Battery.** The boAt lists 100 hours, the realme 38 hours and the OnePlus 28 hours. Fast charging also matters: the boAt lists 24 hours from 10 minutes and the OnePlus 20 hours.
- **Comfort.** The band should rest lightly on your neck. Try it for a few days and return it if it feels heavy.
- **Calls.** Check the number of mics. The boAt lists three mics with AI noise reduction for calls.
- **Water resistance.** Only the realme lists an IP55 rating here.
