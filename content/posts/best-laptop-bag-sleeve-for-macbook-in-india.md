## What to check

- **Size.** Match the sleeve to your laptop size. A 13.6-inch MacBook Air needs a 13.3 to 13.6 inch sleeve.
- **Padding.** Thicker padding protects against bumps. A hard EVA case resists drops better.
- **Water resistance.** Handy in monsoon.
- **Handle or pocket.** A handle and a front pocket help to carry a charger.
