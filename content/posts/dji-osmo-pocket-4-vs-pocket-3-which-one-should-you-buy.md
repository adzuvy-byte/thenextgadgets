## 🎥 Key Differences Explained

### 1. 📸 Better Video Performance (Pocket 4 Wins)

The **DJI Osmo Pocket 4** introduces **4K 240fps**, making it ideal for cinematic slow-motion shots. The **DJI Osmo Pocket 3** is limited to 4K 120fps.

👉 If you shoot reels, travel videos, or cinematic content → Pocket 4 is a clear winner.

### 2. 🎨 Improved Dynamic Range & Color

DJI Osmo Pocket 4 offers **better HDR performance and up to 14 stops dynamic range**, giving more flexibility in editing and color grading.

👉 Creators who edit videos professionally will notice this upgrade.

### 3. 💾 Built-in Storage (Big Upgrade)

One of the biggest advantages of Pocket 4 is **107GB internal storage**.

- No SD card needed
- Faster workflow
- Backup safety

Pocket 3 relies entirely on external memory cards.

### 4. 🤖 Smarter AI Tracking

Pocket 4 features **ActiveTrack 7.0**, which is:

- More accurate
- Better at face tracking
- Smoother for moving subjects

Pocket 3 still performs well, but the upgrade benefits **solo creators and vloggers**.

### 5. 🔋 Battery Life Improvements

Pocket 4 offers slightly better battery performance:

- ~140 mins vs ~116 mins

👉 Not a massive jump, but helpful for long shoots.

### What is the difference between DJI Osmo Pocket 4 and Pocket 3?

### Is DJI Osmo Pocket 4 worth upgrading from Pocket 3?

Upgrade to the **DJI Osmo Pocket 4** if you need better slow motion, improved dynamic range, and internal storage. If you already own the **DJI Osmo Pocket 3**, it’s still good for casual use.

The **DJI Osmo Pocket 4** is better for vlogging due to improved video quality, smoother stabilization, and advanced tracking. The **DJI Osmo Pocket 3** is still great for beginners.

### Which camera is better for beginners, Pocket 3 or Pocket 4?

The **DJI Osmo Pocket 3** is better for beginners due to its lower price and simpler features, while the **DJI Osmo Pocket 4** is ideal for advanced creators.
