## Which one should you pick?

- **Longest playtime and loudest:** Tribit XSound Plus 2 (24 hours, 30W).
- **Best for calls and rugged use:** Sony SRS-XB100 (hands-free calling, IP67).
- **Newest and toughest rating:** JBL Go 5 (IP68, Auracast).
- **Pocket-sized on a budget:** JBL Go 4.
- **More bass in a bigger body:** JBL Flip Essential 2.

## What to check before you buy

- **Water rating.** IP67 and IP68 mean they survive a dip; IPX7 survives short immersion but gives no dust claim in the number itself. The IPX figure alone says nothing about dust.
- **Battery.** Listed hours are at moderate volume. Loud playback uses it faster.
- **Mic.** Two of the JBLs here are sold without a mic, so they cannot take speakerphone calls.
- **Size and sound.** A bigger speaker moves more air and sounds fuller. A palm-sized speaker is easier to carry but thinner in bass.
- **Charging.** USB-C charging is handy. Check the listing for the charging port.
- **Stereo pairing.** Many speakers can pair in pairs for a stereo effect. Check the model you pick.
