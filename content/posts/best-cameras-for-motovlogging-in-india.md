## What matters for motovlogging

- **Stabilisation.** Bike vibration ruins shaky footage.
- **Audio.** Wind noise is the big problem. Plan a mic or a windscreen.
- **Mounting.** Helmet and handlebar mounts are the main cost. Check the mount ecosystem.
- **Battery.** Carry a spare and charge it on long rides.
- **Rugged build.** Rain and drops are common.
