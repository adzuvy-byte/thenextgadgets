## Choosing a Digitek light

- **Desk and phone:** DRL-14 or DRL-14C.
- **Studio and standing shots:** DRL-18RT or DRL-18.
- **On-the-go selfies:** DRL-3W.
- **Colour effects:** the RGB models, or the LED-D6W video light.

## What to check

- **Power.** USB-powered models are portable, AC-powered ones are brighter.
- **Stand height.** Taller stands help standing shots.
- **Colour temperature.** Dual or bi-colour lets you match the room.
- **Remote.** Handy for solo shooting.
