## How to choose

- **Capacity.** 165 to 190L suits one to three people.
- **Star rating.** A 4 or 5 star saves more power. Compare the price gap with the yearly saving.
- **Direct cool.** Cheaper, but you must defrost it manually.
- **Inverter.** Quieter and more efficient.
- **Warranty.** Compare compressor and product warranties.
