## Why you should carry a tyre inflator

Owning a portable tyre inflator is much more than a “nice-to-have” car accessory. Here are key reasons it makes sense:

### Necessity & benefits

- **Emergency preparedness**: On Indian roads you might find yourself with low tyre pressure or a puncture far from a service station. A good inflator means you can restore pressure yourself, and drive safely to the nearest repair point.
- **Fuel efficiency & tyre life**: Under-inflated tyres increase rolling resistance, reduce fuel economy, and cause uneven wear — shortening tyre lifespan. Keeping pressure in check helps avoid this.
- **Safety**: Proper tyre pressure improves handling, braking, and stability. Low or uneven pressure can lead to poor control, particularly on highways or uneven surfaces.
- **Convenience**: Instead of relying on pump stations (which may be absent or slow) you have your own tool.
- **Versatility**: Many inflators also work for bikes, inflatables (balls, air mattresses) etc — adding utility beyond just your car.

### Basic uses

- Checking tyre pressure regularly (ideally once a month or before long trips).
- Topping up pressure when you notice low read-out or after long drives.
- Emergency inflation after a slow leak/puncture so you can reach a repair shop.
- Inflating other items (sports gear, two-wheelers, etc) if your model supports it.

## FAQ: Best Tyre Inflator for Car in India

**What is the ideal PSI range for a car tyre?**The ideal PSI (pounds per square inch) range for a car tyre varies based on the make and model of the car. However, the recommended range is usually between 30-35 PSI.

**Can a car tyre inflator be used for other inflatables?**Yes, car tyre inflators come with adapters that allow you to inflate other items such as sports balls, bicycles, and inflatable pools.

**Can a car tyre inflator overinflate a tyre?**Yes, a car tyre inflator can overinflate a tyre if not used properly. It's important to read the instructions carefully and check the pressure regularly while inflating the tyre.

**How long does it take to inflate a car tyre using a car tyre inflator?**The time taken to inflate a car tyre using a tyre inflator depends on the size of the tyre and the power of the inflator. Typically, it can take anywhere from 3 to 8 minutes.

**Is it safe to use a car tyre inflator in the rain?**No, it is not safe to use a car tyre inflator in the rain as it can cause a short circuit and damage the device. Always use the inflator in a dry and safe environment.

**Can a car tyre inflator be used to repair a flat tyre?**No, a car tyre inflator cannot repair a flat tyre. It can only be used to inflate a tyre. If you have a flat tyre, you will need to repair or replace it.
