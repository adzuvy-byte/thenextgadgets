## What to check

- **Size and airline rules.** Many airlines allow up to about 40 to 45 litres as cabin baggage. Check yours.
- **Laptop fit.** Check the laptop compartment size against your laptop.
- **Padding and straps.** Padded straps and a back panel matter on long days.
- **Zips and fabric.** Water resistance and strong zips help.
- **Weight.** Empty weight eats into your allowance.
