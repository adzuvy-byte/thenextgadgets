## Buying Guide - Best Gaming Laptops under 80000 in India

If you're looking for a gaming laptop under 80000 in India, there are plenty of options available in the market. Here are some things to consider before making your purchase:

1. **Processor**: Look for a laptop with a powerful processor like Intel Core i5 or i7 or AMD Ryzen 5 or 7. The higher the processor speed, the better the gaming experience.
2. **Graphics Card**: A dedicated graphics card is essential for gaming. Look for laptops with Nvidia GTX or RTX or AMD Radeon graphics card.
3. **RAM**: At least 8GB RAM is required for a smooth gaming experience. Look for laptops with 16GB or higher for better performance.
4. **Display**: A high-resolution display with a high refresh rate is recommended for gaming. Look for laptops with Full HD or higher resolution and at least a 120Hz refresh rate.
5. **Storage**: Look for laptops with SSD storage as it offers faster boot times and game loading.
6. **Cooling**: Gaming laptops tend to heat up quickly, so look for laptops with good cooling systems to avoid overheating and performance issues.
7. **Brand**: Choose a reputable brand with good after-sales support and warranty.

## FAQs

**What should I look for in a gaming laptop?**When shopping for a gaming laptop, examine the processor, graphics card, RAM, storage space, and display quality. A gaming laptop should have a powerful processor, dedicated graphics card, at least 8GB of RAM, and an SSD for speedier load times.
