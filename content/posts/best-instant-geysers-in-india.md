## How to choose

- **Tank size.** 3 litres suits a kitchen sink or hand wash. Go bigger for bathroom use.
- **Pressure rating.** Check the bar rating for your building's water pressure.
- **Tank material.** Stainless steel lasts longer than plastic.
- **Power.** 3kW heats quickly but needs a proper socket and wiring.

## What to check

- **Installation.** Get a qualified electrician.
- **Warranty.** 2 year product warranty is common.
- **Safety.** ISI mark and auto cut-off.
