## 1. Video Quality

- **NEXDIGITRON ACE 2**: The ACE 2 features a Full HD 1080p camera with Sony’s STARVIS sensor technology, which delivers excellent low-light performance. This sensor is ideal for capturing clear images even in poor lighting conditions, making it a great choice for night driving.
- **70mai A310**: The A310 also offers 1080p Full HD recording and utilizes a wide-angle lens, though it lacks STARVIS technology. It performs well during the day but may not match the ACE 2’s clarity in low-light or night conditions.

**Winner**: The NEXDIGITRON ACE 2 takes the lead with its superior night vision, thanks to the STARVIS sensor.

## 3. Additional Features

- **NEXDIGITRON ACE 2**: Equipped with GPS, Wi-Fi connectivity, motion detection, and a G-sensor, this dashcam is built with a complete set of features for both security and convenience. GPS tracking is especially helpful for those who need route information in case of incidents.

## Conclusion: Which Dashcam is Best?

Both the NEXDIGITRON ACE 2 and 70mai A310 offer solid performance and essential dashcam features, but the ACE 2 takes the lead in several key areas. With its superior night vision, GPS functionality, and durability, the NEXDIGITRON ACE 2 is ideal for those looking for comprehensive security and high-quality recording. The 70mai A310, on the other hand, remains a good budget-friendly option but may lack some of the advanced features and durability the ACE 2 provides.

If you need a dashcam that can handle low-light situations and provide detailed tracking information, the NEXDIGITRON ACE 2 is the clear winner. For those with a tighter budget who can compromise on GPS and low-light quality, the 70mai A310 is still a strong contender.
