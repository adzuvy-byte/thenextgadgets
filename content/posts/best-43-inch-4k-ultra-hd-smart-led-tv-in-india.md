## About each TV

Prices and ratings were checked on Amazon.in on 2026-11-09 and change often. The descriptions follow what each listing states. We have not measured picture quality in a lab, so brightness and processing claims are the brands' own.

### Xiaomi Smartchoice X 43" 4K Google TV (₹24,999)

A 4K (3840 x 2160) Google TV at 60Hz with Dolby Vision, an eye care mode and dual-band Wi-Fi. It has three HDMI ports including eARC, two USB ports, Bluetooth, optical out and Ethernet, plus 30W of sound with Dolby Audio and DTS-X. It is rated 4.0 from 483 buyers.

Who it suits: buyers who want Dolby Vision and good connectivity at a mid-range price. The rating base is smaller than the Xiaomi FX Pro's.

### Xiaomi FX Pro 43" 4K QLED Fire TV (₹25,999)

A QLED panel with HDR10+, Filmmaker Mode and 30W box speakers. It runs Fire TV rather than Google TV, with three HDMI ports and two USB ports. It is rated 4.0 from 2,460 buyers.

Who it suits: buyers who prefer the Fire TV interface and Prime Video. The ₹1,000 premium over the Smartchoice X buys a QLED panel but swaps Google TV for Fire TV.

### VW Visio World SmartChoice Pro 43" 4K QLED Google TV (₹19,999)

The cheapest 4K QLED here. It has a 60Hz panel, three HDMI ports with eARC, two USB ports, dual-band Wi-Fi, Bluetooth 5.1 and 50W of sound with Dolby Audio. It is rated 4.0 from 2,843 buyers.

Who it suits: buyers on a budget who want QLED and strong speaker output. VW is a newer name than Samsung or LG, so check service coverage in your city.

### Acer Ultra I 43" 4K Google TV (₹20,999)

A 4K LED Google TV with a 178-degree viewing angle and HDMI 2.1 ports, three of them. The listing also mentions MEMC and ALLM, which help with motion and low-latency gaming, and 30W sound with Dolby Audio. It is rated 3.6 from 352 buyers, the lowest here.

Who it suits: people who want HDMI 2.1 connections for a console or PC at a low price. The lower rating means you should read the complaints first.

### Samsung 43" Crystal UHD Vision AI (₹30,490)

A 4K Crystal UHD panel with Vision AI, 4K upscaling, HDR10+ and 30W speakers. It has three HDMI ports, one USB-A, Wi-Fi 5 and Bluetooth 5.3, and a 50Hz refresh rate. It is rated 4.1 from 1,118 buyers.

Who it suits: buyers who want Samsung's Tizen interface, picture processing and service network. The 50Hz panel is a drawback for fast sports and gaming.

### LG 43" NU87 AI Nano 4K webOS (₹31,990)

A Nano 4K LG with webOS, a 60Hz native panel, 178-degree viewing angles, three HDMI ports with eARC and ALLM, one USB 2.0 port, Wi-Fi 5 and Bluetooth 5.3. It is rated 3.9 from 5,494 buyers, the largest rating base on this list.

Who it suits: buyers who like webOS and LG's simple remote. It costs about the same as the Samsung, so compare the interface and service in your area.

### Sony BRAVIA 2M2 43" 4K Google TV (₹46,990)

The premium pick. It is a 60Hz 4K Google TV with four HDMI ports, two USB ports and 20W open-baffle speakers with Dolby Atmos and DTS:X support. It is rated 4.5 from 1,775 buyers, the best on this list.

Who it suits: buyers who care about picture processing and long-term reliability and are willing to pay almost double the budget models. Its speakers are lower wattage than the rest, so a soundbar helps.

## How to choose

- **Panel.** QLED panels are brighter than basic LED. Sony and LG focus on picture processing.
- **OS.** Google TV has the most apps. webOS is simple.
- **HDR.** Dolby Vision and HDR10+ matter for streaming.
- **Sound.** 30W is usual. Add a soundbar for movies.
- **Viewing distance.** At 43 inches, 4K helps if you sit within 6 to 8 feet.
- **Service.** Big brands cost more but are easier to service.

## What to know before buying a 43-inch TV

- **At this size, 4K needs the right distance.** On a 43-inch screen, 4K detail is clearest within about 6 to 8 feet. Beyond that you are paying for pixels you will not see.
- **QLED and LED are not the whole story.** QLED panels use a quantum-dot layer for colour and brightness, and entry-level versions still lack local dimming. Picture processing from Sony and LG can matter more than the panel label.
- **Refresh rate matters for sports and games.** The Samsung is listed at 50Hz and the others at 60Hz. If you game, look for HDMI 2.1 and ALLM.
- **Pick the operating system you will enjoy.** Google TV has the widest app support, Fire TV centres Amazon, and webOS and Tizen are simple and fast. They are hard to change later.
- **Speakers are basic at this size.** Even 50W box speakers will not match a soundbar. Check that the TV has eARC or an optical port.
- **Service and warranty.** Look at the panel warranty and whether the brand has a service centre in your city.

## Should you spend more?

A 43-inch 4K TV at ₹20,000 to ₹30,000 is good for streaming and cable. If you watch films in a dark room, a TV with better contrast and local dimming is a larger upgrade than a bigger screen.
