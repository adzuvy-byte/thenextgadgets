## How to choose

- **Panel.** QLED panels are brighter than basic LED. Sony and LG focus on picture processing.
- **OS.** Google TV has the most apps. webOS is simple.
- **HDR.** Dolby Vision and HDR10+ matter for streaming.
- **Sound.** 30W is usual. Add a soundbar for movies.
- **Viewing distance.** At 43 inches, 4K helps if you sit within 6 to 8 feet.
- **Service.** Big brands cost more but are easier to service.
