## Product-by-product notes

**Philips Viva Collection HD4928.** The safe choice, with more than 57,000 ratings and a 4.2 average. It is a 2100 W unit with five presets and Smart Sense, which detects whether a suitable vessel is on the cooktop. At around ₹3,329 it costs more than the budget models, and you pay for the brand and track record.

**Pigeon Cruise 1800 W.** The cheapest here at around ₹1,678 and by far the most reviewed, with 77,000 ratings, though the average is 3.7. It has a crystal glass top, an LED display and auto switch-off, with a 1-year warranty. A good first induction cooktop, but go in expecting basic build.

**Prestige PIC 20.** A 1600 W cooktop with eight Indian presets such as pressure cook, curry and dosa/chapati, plus 4 KV surge protection and an automatic voltage regulator. Around ₹2,099 and rated 4.0 by about 44,800 buyers. Sensible for homes with unsteady voltage.

**iBELL 20YO.** A 2000 W cooktop with full touch control and a crystal glass top, rated 4.2 by about 4,300 buyers. The listing offers a 3-year warranty, but the last two years depend on registering the product. A fast, simple option at around ₹2,290.

**Prestige Iris Sleek.** A 2000 W Prestige with individual touch buttons, 4 KV surge protection and eight presets. It costs about ₹2,995 and has 658 ratings, so you pay more for the brand and the protection.

**Longway Ember Plus.** The cheapest 2000 W model on the list at around ₹1,399, with seven presets and push-button controls. It is rated 4.0 by 314 buyers, and the 2-year warranty needs registration. Good value, but the brand is less established.

**Usha Cook Joy 3616.** A 1600 W cooktop with a pan sensor that cuts power when no vessel is present, five presets and protection against voltage fluctuations up to 1500 V. It has about 11,800 ratings at 4.2, which is a stronger record than most of the budget models. The warranty is one year.

**Bosch TrueSafe 2100 W.** The premium option, with 6 kV surge protection, a touch panel and eight presets. At around ₹4,953 it is the most expensive here, and it has only 140 ratings so far. Consider it if you live where voltage is unstable and you want a well-known brand.


## What to check

- **Wattage.** 1600 to 2000W covers most cooking. Higher watts boil faster but trip older house wiring.
- **Cookware.** Induction needs magnetic pans such as steel or cast iron. Aluminium does not work unless it has an induction base.
- **Voltage protection.** Surge protection helps with unstable supply.
- **Controls.** Touch buttons are sleek but knobs are more forgiving with wet hands.
- **Cooling fan.** The fan is loud and clogs with dust, so keep the vents clear.
