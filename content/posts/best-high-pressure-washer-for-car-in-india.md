## How to choose a pressure washer for a car

- **Pressure (bar).** 100 to 140 bar is enough for cars and bikes. Much higher pressure can damage paint, rubber seals and trim.
- **Flow rate.** Flow, in litres per minute or per hour, decides how quickly dirt is flushed away. Aim for roughly 6 L/min or more.
- **Motor wattage.** Wattage shows power draw, not cleaning ability. Compare it with pressure and flow instead of relying on it alone.
- **Hose and cord.** A hose of 5 m or more and a long cord let you walk around the car without moving the machine.
- **Self-priming.** A self-priming washer can draw water from a bucket, useful if you have no tap pressure.
- **Accessories.** A foam lance or foam bottle, adjustable nozzle and a gun that fits well matter more than the wattage.
- **Care.** Keep the nozzle about 30 cm from paint, avoid sensitive areas, and drain the pump after use.
- **Maintenance.** Store it away from frost and run clean water through it occasionally.
