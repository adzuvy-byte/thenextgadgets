## True 4K + 2.5K + 1080P Recording — Triple Channel Protection

The **Redtiger F17 Elite** captures everything around your car in exceptional clarity. Equipped with the **Sony STARVIS 2 IMX678** sensor on the front camera and **IMX675** sensors for the rear and interior lenses, this dash cam records at **4K (2160p)** front, **2.5K rear**, and **1080p interior**, simultaneously.

This 3-channel setup ensures comprehensive coverage — perfect for taxi, rideshare, or long-distance drivers who need full front, cabin, and rear protection. The new **STARVIS 2 technology** enhances light sensitivity dramatically, producing bright, noise-free videos even in dim environments or complete darkness.

Additionally, its **HDR (High Dynamic Range)** and **Full Night Color** support ensure balanced exposure and vivid color reproduction, preventing glare from headlights or overexposure during sunny drives.

## Full-Color Night Vision with STARVIS 2 Sensors

One of the biggest challenges with dash cams is nighttime performance. Many produce grainy or overexposed footage. The **Redtiger F17 Elite** uses **Sony STARVIS 2 sensors** with **AI-based imaging** that delivers **full-color footage even in near-darkness**. Instead of black-and-white infrared, you’ll see vibrant, clear details—an advantage for identifying people, number plates, and road conditions at night.

## High-Speed Wi-Fi 6 & Easy App Control

Most dash cams suffer from slow file transfers to your phone. The F17 Elite solves this with **Wi-Fi 6 (5.8 GHz)**, enabling **up to 30 MB/s transfer speeds**. This makes it easy to quickly review or download clips via the **Redtiger App** without removing the memory card.

## Build Quality, Design & Installation

The **Redtiger F17 Elite** features a premium, sleek build that blends seamlessly into the windshield. Despite packing three cameras, it maintains a compact footprint. The front cam records through the windshield, while the rear and cabin units can be easily mounted using adhesive 3M tapes.

The inclusion of a **128GB microSD card** in the box is a big plus — most brands skip this, forcing you to purchase one separately. The F17 supports loop recording, automatically overwriting old footage to save space without missing important moments.

Thanks to its **efficient heat dissipation design**, the dashcam performs well even under direct sunlight or during long drives, maintaining stable video performance throughout.
