## How to choose

- **ANC.** Fine for commutes. The depth varies, so check dB where stated.
- **Battery.** 40 to 70 hours (with the case) is common now.
- **Calls.** Quad mics and ENC help in noisy places.
- **Latency.** Low-latency mode helps with gaming and video.
- **Fit.** Try different ear tips.

## How to judge budget earbuds

- **Read "total playtime" correctly.** Figures like 70 or 75 hours include the charging case. A single charge of the earbuds themselves is usually 4 to 8 hours, so check that number if you listen for long stretches.
- **ANC strength is not everything.** The decibel figure is the brand's peak claim. Fit matters more than the number, because a poor seal leaks noise. Try the different ear tips.
- **Calls need good microphones.** Quad-mic and ENC systems help in traffic, but your own voice quality varies. Look for reviews from callers in noisy places.
- **Low latency helps video and games.** Figures such as 45 ms matter if you game on your phone. For music alone, they matter less.
- **Water resistance is for sweat and rain.** IPX5 and IP55 handle workouts and light rain. They do not cover swimming or showers.
- **Check the app and warranty.** A companion app gives you equaliser and ANC controls. Look at the warranty period and how hard service is in your city.

## Should you spend more?

Above ₹2,000 you get better drivers, stronger ANC and more refined call quality. If you use earbuds for hours every day on trains or in an office, it may be worth stretching. For commuting, workouts and everyday listening, the earbuds above are good value.
