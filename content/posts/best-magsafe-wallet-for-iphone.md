## What to check

- **Card capacity.** Three cards is slim. Five or six is bulky.
- **Magnet strength.** Cheap wallets can slip off. Read reviews.
- **Wireless charging.** Remove the wallet or cards when charging. Thick wallets block it.
- **RFID blocking.** Useful for contactless cards.
- **Material.** PU leather wears faster than real leather.
